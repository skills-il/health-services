# Domain Coverage Checklist, israeli-celiac-navigator

Generated: 2026-07-26, via Kol-Zchut celiac rights page, Schneider Children's
Medical Center celiac article, Leumit celiac nutrition guide, and the Bituach
Leumi disabled-child impairment list.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] Pre-test gluten rule, the defining fact of the domain, source: Schneider, why core: a gluten-free diet started before biopsy diagnosis may mislead and obstruct diagnosis. Getting this wrong costs the family either a deliberate gluten challenge or an unconfirmed lifelong diet
- [x] Handling the already-gluten-free family WITHOUT prescribing reintroduction, source: Schneider, why core: the reintroduction plan is a medical decision. The skill must route it, not design it
- [x] Serology panel, IgA group anti-TTG and anti-endomysium, source: Schneider, why core: names what is actually ordered
- [x] IgA-deficiency caveat, source: Schneider, why core: IgA deficiency is MORE common in celiac patients, so a lone negative result is not an all-clear. Omitting this produces false reassurance
- [x] Biopsy as diagnostic basis AND the two repeat-biopsy triggers (uncertain first diagnosis, or diagnosis before age two), source: Schneider, why core: parents of early-diagnosed toddlers assume the question is closed. Since 2026-10-29 the repeat biopsy is framed as the gastroenterologist's decision, because the Israeli paediatric guideline does not list the under-two trigger
- [x] Exceptional non-biopsy route (a decision for the treating gastroenterologist under the guideline's criteria, which the skill deliberately does not restate; never for asymptomatic children), source: Israeli paediatric guideline s.3.3, added 2026-10-29, why core: a family diagnosed on serology alone otherwise reads the skill as saying its diagnosis is incomplete
- [x] Low IgA means IgG-type serology, source: Israeli paediatric guideline s.2.1
- [x] Education entitlement, named instrument (MoE standing instruction 0404 of 01.08.2023), craft materials inside the obligation, birthdays / food sales / mishloach manot, long-day meals, non-exclusion, parents' health declaration, source: Kol-Zchut education sub-page, added 2026-10-29
- [x] Education entitlement, ALL FOUR components enumerated, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: substitutes in every activity inside OR outside the institution; gluten-free offering wherever food is supplied or sold; portions in a different colour with special prominent marking; parent duty to inform before the year. A row saying "covers school rights" would pass while omitting the colour-marking mechanism that actually prevents mix-ups
- [x] Gluten labelling, both required elements, source: the 1996 gluten regulation (amended 2011) and Kol-Zchut, why core: plant source AND the words מכיל גלוטן, in the ingredient list, for food above 20 PPM
- [x] Labelling TRANSITION to the EU 1169/2011 allergen format (grain emphasised inside the ingredient list, separate box replaced, "מכיל גלוטן" only recommended, voluntary from 1.1.2025, mandatory from 1.1.2028; bulk food deferred to 1.1.2032), source: MoH allergen guide v2 29/06/2025 and MoE 2026 gan model, added 2026-10-29, why core: the old shelf method ("scan for מכיל גלוטן / the allergens box") finds nothing on a compliant new-format wheat pack. Temporal dimension: two formats coexist until 2028, so both must be taught
- [x] Spelt (כוסמין) as wheat, and the כוסמין / כוסמת confusion, source: MoE gan model, MoH allergen guide, added 2026-10-29
- [x] Medication, BOTH points, source: Kol-Zchut and Leumit, why core: check for wheat-derived flour or starch; and the right to a suitable alternative when the basket drug is unsuitable
- [x] Material assistance, BOTH age bands, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: up to retirement age via social services with physician recommendation; and separately at retirement age. Both budget-bound and priority-ranked per local authority, so never promised. Published ceiling (up to 1,210 NIS a month as of 2026, co-payment never below 10 percent) and the appeal route added 2026-10-29
- [x] Income tax negative: section 45 credit points for a child with a disability follow the disabled-child allowance, so celiac alone does not open them, source: Kol-Zchut tax-credit sub-page, added 2026-10-29
- [x] Subsidised gluten-free flour (Health Ministry), reported at the Knesset Health Committee 11.05.2026, source: Maariv (secondary), added 2026-10-29 with no price
- [x] Bituach Leumi negative finding, source: BTL impairment list, why core: celiac is NOT a named criterion while allergy IS. Without this stated explicitly, an agent generalises from the sibling allergy skill and invents an entitlement
- [x] IDF track, all sub-dimensions, sources: Kol-Zchut and the IDF's own page (mitgaisim.idf.il, 27.11.2022), why core: profile 97 with celiac clause; closed per IDF: submarine flotilla and עוצבת האש (paratroopers, commando) except עוצבת קלע דוד; Kol-Zchut additionally: reconnaissance apart from pilot / naval officers / naval combat; manoeuvring combat open on a volunteer basis only; named open roles; gluten-free rations
- [x] IDF stipend, CORRECTED 2026-10-29: the IDF page DOES publish it, 21.9 NIS a day open base and 34.9 NIS a day closed base as of 27.11.2022, and Kol-Zchut says it may change. Quote it with its date; the previous "deliberately not stated, not published" row was wrong (the skill relied on Kol-Zchut only and never opened the IDF page it links to)
- [x] Adult newly diagnosed entry route, added 2026-10-29
- [x] Celiac is not IgE allergy, boundary stated explicitly, why core: prevents the anaphylaxis playbook being imported

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] Prevalence, about one percent plus the Israeli 1:157 study, source: Schneider, why: reassurance and context
- [x] Grain list including the contested status of oats, source: Leumit
- [x] Cross-contamination during harvest or milling for quinoa, teff, amaranth, sorghum, source: Leumit, why: explains why manufacturer confirmation beats botanical reasoning
- [x] Pesach in BOTH directions, source: Leumit, why: kosher-for-Passover is not gluten-free (matza flour), but Passover products without matza or Passover flour CAN BE suitable, after the normal label check
- [x] Kitniyot is a kashrut category and says nothing about gluten, why: the most common vocabulary confusion in this domain
- [x] Label-reading method plus written manufacturer confirmation, source: Leumit
- [x] Government helplines, source: Kol-Zchut
- [x] Birthday parties and the packaged-snack shelf: the four-tier sorting method (including unpackaged food as its own tier), the named traps (Bisli, regular beigele, Click, wafers and biscuits, Pesek Zman, Kif Kif, Kinder Joy, Krembo, KitKat and Twix, ice-cream cones, soup nuts), the same-brand-two-versions pattern, the party table and cake cross-contamination rules, the gan-party link back to the Step 3 entitlement, the unpackaged-food category, and the read-the-pack rule, source: Celiac Rights Organisation plus Osem product line, v1.1.0

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- IgE food allergy, anaphylaxis, EpiPen, related skill: `israeli-food-allergy-navigator`
- Meal planning, recipes, menu building. Re-litigated 2026-10-29: users do ask, constantly, but `israeli-nutrition-planner` excludes therapeutic diets, so the first destination is the kupat cholim dietitian (Step 2.5), which is the treatment itself. Stays out of scope; the skill routes to the dietitian first
- Clinical diagnosis, serology interpretation, designing a gluten challenge, medical scope, route to a gastroenterologist
- General Bituach Leumi benefits, related skill: `israeli-bituach-leumi`
- Kashrut certification generally, related skill: `israeli-food-business-compliance` for the business side
- Non-Israeli celiac systems (re-litigated 2026-10-29: olim with a foreign diagnosis do ask, but how a kupah treats a foreign diagnosis is not published anywhere this pipeline could read; stays out)
- Per-kupah dietitian copayments and session counts (2026-10-29: only Maccabi's page was readable; the skill names Maccabi's terms as an example and tells the user to check their own kupah)
