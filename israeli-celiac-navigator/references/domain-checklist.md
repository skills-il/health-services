# Domain Coverage Checklist, israeli-celiac-navigator

Generated: 2026-07-26, via Kol-Zchut celiac rights page, Schneider Children's
Medical Center celiac article, Leumit celiac nutrition guide, and the Bituach
Leumi disabled-child impairment list.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] Pre-test gluten rule, the defining fact of the domain, source: Schneider, why core: a gluten-free diet started before biopsy diagnosis may mislead and obstruct diagnosis. Getting this wrong costs the family either a deliberate gluten challenge or an unconfirmed lifelong diet
- [x] Handling the already-gluten-free family WITHOUT prescribing reintroduction, source: Schneider, why core: the reintroduction plan is a medical decision. The skill must route it, not design it
- [x] Serology panel, IgA group anti-TTG and anti-endomysium, source: Schneider, why core: names what is actually ordered
- [x] IgA-deficiency caveat, source: Schneider, why core: IgA deficiency is MORE common in celiac patients, so a lone negative result is not an all-clear. Omitting this produces false reassurance
- [x] Biopsy as diagnostic basis AND the two repeat-biopsy triggers (uncertain first diagnosis, or diagnosis before age two), source: Schneider, why core: parents of early-diagnosed toddlers assume the question is closed
- [x] Education entitlement, ALL FOUR components enumerated, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: substitutes in every activity inside OR outside the institution; gluten-free offering wherever food is supplied or sold; portions in a different colour with special prominent marking; parent duty to inform before the year. A row saying "covers school rights" would pass while omitting the colour-marking mechanism that actually prevents mix-ups
- [x] Gluten labelling, both required elements, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: plant source AND the words מכיל גלוטן, in the ingredient list
- [x] Medication, BOTH points, source: Kol-Zchut and Leumit, why core: check for wheat-derived flour or starch; and the right to a suitable alternative when the basket drug is unsuitable
- [x] Material assistance, BOTH age bands, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: up to retirement age via social services with physician recommendation; and separately at retirement age. Both budget-bound and priority-ranked per local authority, so never promised
- [x] Bituach Leumi negative finding, source: BTL impairment list, why core: celiac is NOT a named criterion while allergy IS. Without this stated explicitly, an agent generalises from the sibling allergy skill and invents an entitlement
- [x] IDF track, all four sub-dimensions, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: profile 97 with celiac clause; excluded units (commando, paratroopers, submarine flotilla, and reconnaissance apart from pilot / naval officers / naval combat); open field roles; stipend in addition to dining-hall provision, varying by open or closed base
- [x] IDF stipend amount deliberately NOT stated, source: Kol-Zchut, why core: the source explicitly says the amount varies and is not fixed. Inventing a figure is the exact failure this skill guards against
- [x] Celiac is not IgE allergy, boundary stated explicitly, why core: prevents the anaphylaxis playbook being imported

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] Prevalence, about one percent plus the Israeli 1:157 study, source: Schneider, why: reassurance and context
- [x] Grain list including the contested status of oats, source: Leumit
- [x] Cross-contamination during harvest or milling for quinoa, teff, amaranth, sorghum, source: Leumit, why: explains why manufacturer confirmation beats botanical reasoning
- [x] Pesach in BOTH directions, source: Leumit, why: kosher-for-Passover is not gluten-free (matza flour), but Passover products without matza or Passover flour ARE suitable
- [x] Kitniyot is a kashrut category and says nothing about gluten, why: the most common vocabulary confusion in this domain
- [x] Label-reading method plus written manufacturer confirmation, source: Leumit
- [x] Government helplines, source: Kol-Zchut
- [ ] Named gluten-free product brands and prices, deliberately NOT included, they change constantly; pair with the `supermarket-prices` MCP instead

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- IgE food allergy, anaphylaxis, EpiPen, related skill: `israeli-food-allergy-navigator`
- Meal planning, recipes, menu building, related skill: `israeli-nutrition-planner`
- Clinical diagnosis, serology interpretation, designing a gluten challenge, medical scope, route to a gastroenterologist
- General Bituach Leumi benefits, related skill: `israeli-bituach-leumi`
- Kashrut certification generally, related skill: `israeli-food-business-compliance` for the business side
- Non-Israeli celiac systems
