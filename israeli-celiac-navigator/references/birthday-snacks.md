# Birthday-party snacks and sweets, gluten-free shortlist (Israel)

**What this file is.** A shortlist of packaged snacks and sweets that Israeli celiac
families commonly buy for birthday parties. It was compiled from a parents'
community list, which is unverified user-generated content, not manufacturer
certification. It is a starting point for the supermarket aisle and nothing more.

**What this file is not.** It is not a certification. No row below clears a product.
The only thing that clears a product is the marking on the specific pack in your
hand, checked on the day. Recipes, flavourings and production lines change, brands
add and drop gluten-free versions, and a snack range is rarely uniform across all
its flavours.

**The rule that overrides every row.** Under Israeli labelling rules a product
containing gluten must name the source plant in the ingredient list and state
`מכיל גלוטן`. So the method is: find the product on the shelf, turn the pack over,
read the ingredient list, and buy it only if it is clean. A remembered product name
is never sufficient. When a pack is unclear, ask the manufacturer and get the answer
in writing.

**Never treat `כשר לפסח` as a gluten-free marking.** Kosher-for-Passover products are
routinely made from matza flour, which is wheat. See Step 5 of the skill.

For anything beyond a one-off party purchase, use the Celiac organisation's
maintained food guide, which tracks thousands of products continuously. This file
cannot. Review this file at each skill update and re-verify any row a family relies
on repeatedly.

Compiled 2026-07-27.

## Savoury

| Product | Community status | What decides at the shelf |
|---|---|---|
| Bamba (במבה) | Commonly used | Corn and peanut base with no flour in the recipe, but Osem also produces wheat snacks, so confirm the pack carries no `מכיל גלוטן` |
| Apropo (אפרופו) | Commonly used | Multiple variants exist; check the specific flavour's ingredient list |
| Cheetos (צ'יטוס) | Commonly used | Corn base, but the seasoning is where wheat enters. Check flavour by flavour, never the range as a whole |
| Doritos (דוריטוס) | Commonly used | Same as Cheetos: corn base, per-flavour seasoning. Check the specific bag |
| Dubonim (דובונים) | Commonly used | Check the specific flavour's ingredient list |
| Tapuchips (תפוצ'יפס) | Commonly used, **except the baked variety** | Potato base; the baked variety is excluded by the community list. Check the specific bag |
| Beigele (בייגלה) | Only the marked gluten-free line | Osem sells a beigele explicitly marked `ללא גלוטן` beside the regular wheat one. Only the pack marked `ללא גלוטן` is suitable. A Passover marking does not substitute |

**Commonly contain gluten, treat as out unless a pack says otherwise:** Bisli (wheat
flour is the leading ingredient at roughly 80 to 85 percent), regular beigele,
Kabukim, soup nuts (שקדי מרק), bourekas, pizza, malawach, and any battered or
crumbed platter item.

## Sweet

| Product | Community status | What decides at the shelf |
|---|---|---|
| Para chocolate (שוקולד פרה) | Only the marked varieties | Many varieties; buy only those marked `ללא גלוטן` on the front of the pack |
| Wafer rolls (אפיפיות) | Only the gluten-free version | Elite or Shufersal gluten-free version only; the regular one is a wafer |
| Red Bamba (במבה אדומה) | Commonly used | Check the pack as with regular Bamba |
| Elite mini lentils (מיני עדשים) | Commonly used | Check the pack |
| Mekupelet (מקופלת) | Commonly used | Sold in several variants including filled and coated ones; check the specific bar |
| Petit Beurre (פתיבר) | Only the marked version | Only the pack marked `ללא גלוטן`. The standard biscuit is wheat |
| Shufersal cereal in milk-chocolate coating | Commonly used | Cereal-based, so the grain matters; check the ingredient list |
| Marshmallow (Carmit, Yogota, Shufersal, Tante) | Commonly used | Coatings and starch dusting vary by brand and batch; check the pack |
| Tante gummy sweets | Only packs marked gluten-free | The marking is printed on the wrapper |
| Kinder fingers (אצבעות קינדר) | Commonly used | Check the pack; do not generalise to other Kinder products |
| Kinder Surprise egg | The **regular** egg only, **not** Kinder Joy | Same brand, different product. Read the box, do not infer from the brand |
| M&M's | Plain and peanut varieties are the ones families use | Identify by variant and by the ingredient list, never by pack colour. Crispy, pretzel and cookie variants are not suitable |
| Toffifee, Merci, Reese's, Bounty, Snickers | Commonly used | Imported confectionery; formulations differ by import batch, so read the pack each time |

**Commonly contain gluten, treat as out unless a pack says otherwise:** Click,
regular wafers, assorted cookies and biscuits, Pesek Zman, Kif Kif, Kinder Joy,
Krembo (biscuit base), KitKat and Twix (wafer and biscuit under the chocolate),
ice-cream cones and wafer bases, sufganiyot.

## Anything unpackaged is a separate category

Homemade cake, a platter someone brought, bulk-bin sweets, a bakery cake, a chocolate
fountain: there is no pack, no ingredient list and no traceability. The instruction
for this entire class is different in kind. No marking means not for this child,
regardless of how confident the person offering it is.

## How to use this with a family

1. Lead with the tiers in Step 4b of the skill, not with the product names. A family
   that understands the sorting method can handle a product that is not on this list.
2. Quote at most a handful of relevant rows, always with the read-the-pack rule
   attached.
3. Never state that a named product is certified gluten-free. State that it appears on
   a community shortlist and that the marking on the pack decides.
4. Point the family at the Celiac organisation's maintained food guide for anything
   beyond a one-off party purchase.
