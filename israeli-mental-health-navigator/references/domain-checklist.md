# Domain coverage checklist: Israeli mental health navigation

Anchor for the Expert Review gate. It records WHAT a complete workflow in this domain must cover
and WHY that item is core. It deliberately carries few numbers: a figure belongs in `evidence.json`
with a source, never here, because a checklist row is not a verified claim and a later cycle must
not transcribe one as if it were.

## Must cover (core)

| # | Item | Why it is core |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | The July 2015 transfer of ambulatory mental health from the Ministry of Health to the kupot under the National Health Insurance Law | Determines who owns the entitlement and how a user gets in |
| 2 | Referral and pre-authorization rules for the community route, per kupah | The commonest first obstacle a user hits |
| 3 | Copay BY SETTING, as its own axis: community clinic, general-hospital outpatient clinic, psychiatric-hospital outpatient clinic, MoH public clinic, clinic under agreement | The service catalogue defines the copay by setting, not by kupah. A single national number is always wrong |
| 4 | Copay by KUPAH, for each setting, or an explicit statement that a given kupah's booklet was not read | Three of four kupot publish their secondary-physician tariff only in a copay booklet. Assuming they match the one kupah we did read is the failure this row exists to prevent |
| 5 | Session entitlement by setting: the assessment + treatment-card structure at public and hospital-outpatient clinics versus no session cap at clinics under agreement | Users are routinely told one number for both settings |
| 6 | The agreement-clinic route as the answer to a long waiting list, and the fact that a self-found private therapist is not reimbursed | This is the practical exit from the waiting-list problem |
| 7 | Emergency route: psychiatric ER open 24/7, no referral, no charge; general-hospital ER charges a fee reduced by a Form 17 commitment and waived on admission | The referral rules in rows 2 to 5 do NOT apply here, and applying them to someone in acute distress is the most harmful error this skill can make |
| 8 | Crisis lines with their real hours, distinguishing 24/7 phone lines from text channels with narrow windows, and distinguishing crisis lines from information desks | Hours are load-bearing. A correct number at an hour nobody answers is a failed referral |
| 9 | Involuntary admission under the Treatment of Mental Patients Law, 1991: hospitalization periods and who may extend them, appeal to the psychiatric committee and its window, committee composition, District Court appeal window, right to counsel | The skill's own crisis examples reach involuntary admission; naming it without the safeguards leaves the user with no lever |
| 10 | Therapy modalities available and what the basket funds | Users ask for a modality by name |
| 11 | Private therapy price levels from a methodologically grounded survey, with the professions the survey does NOT cover named as not covered | Quoting a psychiatrist rate from a survey that never sampled psychiatrists is fabrication |
| 12 | Workplace rights: sick-day accrual and the payment ladder, the fact that the certificate carries no diagnosis, and disability-discrimination protection with the accommodation duty | The single most actionable part of the skill for an employed user |
| 13 | PTSD recognition tracks kept distinct: Bituach Leumi general disability, Bituach Leumi hostility victims, MoD Rehabilitation, and the mental-health rehabilitation basket | Four different bodies, four different thresholds. Merging any two produces a wrong entitlement answer |
| 14 | For each track, the ORDER of steps and which form belongs to which step | Form 571 is a post-recognition benefit request, not a way in. Presenting it as the recognition form sends a claimant to a form they cannot use |
| 15 | The rehabilitation-basket threshold and its independence from the disability pension | A person refused a pension may still qualify. Merging the two thresholds denies a real entitlement |

## Should cover (advanced)

| # | Item | Why |
|---|------|-----|
| 16 | Supplementary insurance (shaban) and what it can and cannot sell in this field | Users arrive believing shaban buys adult psychotherapy. Whether and how it may is a live question this skill has not verified and must not assert either way |
| 17 | Children and adolescents: priority placement, and whether clinic-type and copay rules differ for minors | Asked constantly; currently answered only in outline |
| 18 | Waiting times: what is actually binding, as distinct from proposals that circulate as if they were rules | A widely circulated draft standard is not a right, and quoting one as an entitlement sets a user up to demand something they cannot get |
| 19 | Escalation beyond the kupah's own ombudsman, to the Ministry of Health complaints route | The kupah's internal ombudsman is not the last word and users are rarely told so |
| 20 | Psychiatric day hospitalization as the level between outpatient therapy and admission | The skill currently jumps from weekly therapy to the ER |
| 21 | Population-specific routes, including religious, immigrant, Arabic-speaking, Ethiopian-Israeli and LGBTQ+ services | A national directory that omits a population effectively has no answer for it |
| 22 | Resilience centres: who is eligible and on what basis | Free and substantial, and routinely mistaken for a general anxiety service |

## Out of scope (explicit)

Reviewed and re-affirmed 2026-09-02.

| Item | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| Clinical assessment of any kind: diagnosis, severity rating, scoring or interpreting a screening instrument, medication choice, dosing, interactions or discontinuation | Reserved to a physician or a licensed psychologist. Would an ordinary user ask for it? Yes, constantly, which is why the refusal must always come with a route (a hotline, a psychiatric ER, the kupah's mental health department) rather than silence |
| Predicting the disability percentage or pension a user will be awarded | The skill explains thresholds and gates; the determination is a medical committee's. A user does ask this, and the honest answer is the ladder plus who decides, not a number |
| Drafting the substance of a claim, an appeal, or a causation argument | Legal work. The skill may state what documents and deadlines apply |
| Recommending a named therapist, clinic or institute | No basis on which to rank them, and an endorsement carries a duty we cannot discharge |
| Suicide-risk assessment conducted in the conversation | The skill routes to a crisis line or a psychiatric ER and stops |
| A directory of individual district psychiatrists' or psychiatric hospitals' phone numbers | A wrong number in this context is a real-harm failure. Link to the official district and facility pages instead |
| Routes for minors that sit outside the kupah, in particular school-based educational-psychology services | Reviewed 2026-09-02. Would an ordinary user ask? Yes, a parent would. It stays out of scope for now for a specific reason rather than by omission: no source for its terms could be read this cycle (the Kol Zchut page 404s and the gov.il page is bot-blocked), and the rule here is not to write an entitlement we cannot cite. The skill therefore says explicitly that these routes exist outside its scope and points the parent at the school or municipality, rather than asserting coverage it does not deliver. Re-open next cycle with a readable source. |
| Substance-abuse and addiction treatment pathways as a primary subject | A distinct service network. Cross-reference only |
| Non-Israeli mental health systems, and private (non-shaban) insurance policies | Outside the skill's stated scope |

## Authoritative sources

- Ministry of Health mental health portal and its official helpline index: me.health.gov.il
- The four kupot: clalit.co.il, maccabi4u.co.il, meuhedet.co.il, leumit.co.il (their own published entitlement and copay pages, not third-party summaries)
- The National Health Insurance service catalogue entry for the setting-based copay rule: call.gov.il
- Bituach Leumi: btl.gov.il, in particular the hostility-victims and general-disability sections
- MoD Rehabilitation Division: shikum.mod.gov.il
- Treatment of Mental Patients Law, 1991, full text: he.wikisource.org
- Kol Zchut for the taxonomy of named entitlements, used for discovery and never as the sole source for a figure
