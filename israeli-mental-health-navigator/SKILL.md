# Israeli Mental Health Navigator

## Instructions

### Step 1: Mental Health Rights Under National Health Insurance

Since July 2015, mental health care in Israel has been covered under the National Health Insurance Law (chok bituach briut mamlachti, חוק ביטוח בריאות ממלכתי). This landmark reform transferred psychiatric and psychological services from the Ministry of Health to the kupot cholim (health funds).

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Year of reform | July 2015 |
| Previous system | Government-run mental health centers |
| Current system | Kupot cholim (Clalit, Maccabi, Meuhedet, Leumit) |
| What is covered | Psychiatric consultation, psychotherapy, psychological assessment, medication |
| Who is eligible | All Israeli residents registered with a kupat cholim |
| Cost to patient (2026) | Depends on the setting, not on a single national number. See the copay matrix in Step 2. Maccabi's published figures: 41 NIS quarterly at a public-hospital outpatient clinic, free at a psychiatric hospital, free at public or agreement clinics (as of 29.7.2026). |

Key rights:
- Every Israeli resident has the right to mental health treatment through their kupat cholim
- A referral IS required. The Ministry of Health service page instructs: get a referral form from your family doctor, and check with your kupah whether Form 17 is also needed. Maccabi additionally requires a pre-authorization request (bakashat hitchayvut) submitted through its app or portal with the referral attached, before you book
- Treatment is available for children, adolescents, and adults
- Coverage includes both individual and group therapy
- Psychiatric medications are covered under the health basket (sal briut, סל בריאות)

2025-2026 system status: the government approved adding approximately 366 million NIS to the 2025 health basket and an additional 175 million NIS in 2026 to expand mental health services. Kupat cholim mental health budgets jumped from around 347 million NIS in 2024 to around 591 million NIS in 2025. Despite this, demand continues to outpace supply and waiting lists remain a known bottleneck.

### Step 2: Accessing Therapy Through Kupat Cholim

Each of the four kupot cholim provides mental health services. The process for starting therapy is as follows:

| Step | Action | Details |
|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Get a referral | From your family doctor or from a clinician in the mental health system. Ask whether Form 17 is also required |
| 2 | Submit a pre-authorization request | Kupah-dependent. Maccabi requires bakashat hitchayvut via app, online portal, or branch, with the referral attached |
| 3 | Contact the mental health department | Call or visit the machleket briut hanefesh (מחלקת בריאות הנפש) and book directly at the clinic |
| 4 | Initial assessment | The diagnostic stage is a defined entitlement, not an informal screening. See the session table below |
| 5 | Begin treatment | Typically weekly sessions, reviewed periodically |

Copay depends on the SETTING, not on which kupah you belong to. The National Health Insurance service catalogue defines it by reference:

| Setting | Copay rule |
|---------|-----------|
| Community mental health clinic (מרפאה בקהילה) | Charged at the secondary-physician (rofeh shniyoni) quarterly tariff |
| Outpatient clinic in a general hospital (מרפאת חוץ בבית חולים כללי) | Charged at the standard hospital outpatient tariff |
| Outpatient clinic in a psychiatric hospital (מרפאת חוץ בבית חולים פסיכיאטרי) | Charged at the secondary-physician quarterly tariff |

The only per-kupah figures published in a form we could read directly are Maccabi's (page last updated 29.7.2026): **41 NIS quarterly** at a public-hospital outpatient clinic, **free** at a psychiatric hospital, **free** at public Ministry of Health clinics and at private clinics under agreement with Maccabi.

VERIFY BEFORE QUOTING: Clalit, Meuhedet and Leumit publish their secondary-physician quarterly tariffs in separate copay booklets (alon hishtatfuyot atzmiyot) that we have not read. Do not assume they match Maccabi's 41 NIS. Direct the user to their own kupah's published booklet or service line.

Session entitlement (Maccabi's published structure, at Ministry of Health public clinics and hospital outpatient clinics):

| Stage | Entitlement |
|-------|-------------|
| Assessment (adults or children) | 2 sessions: one diagnostic meeting plus one treatment meeting |
| Treatment card (kartisiya, כרטיסייה) | 15 therapeutic sessions from a defined list, up to one year from the first treatment, whichever comes first |
| At private clinics under agreement with the kupah | Assessment plus a course of treatment at the therapist's clinical discretion. No fixed session cap |

The agreement-clinic route is the one that has no session cap and no cost. It is usually the best answer for someone who cannot afford private therapy.

Important notes:
- Wait times can be 2-8 weeks (and longer in many regions since 2023). The 2025 Ministry of Health funding report shows treatment volume grew substantially but demand still outpaces supply.
- The session structure is the 2 + 15 kartisiya described above, not an informal 12-16 range
- If the assigned therapist is not a good fit, you have the right to request a different one
- Supplementary insurance (shaban, שב"ן) may provide additional sessions or faster access
- Children and adolescents often receive priority placement
- Do NOT expect reimbursement for a therapist you find on your own. Maccabi states plainly that no reimbursement is given for private mental health services. The correct route when the public clinic is full is a referral to a private clinic UNDER AGREEMENT with your kupah (מרפאה פרטית שבהסכם), where treatment length is at the therapist's clinical discretion at no cost to you.
- Once a course of treatment has started, a kupah may not cut it off because of a contract dispute with the provider (Deputy Director General circular 01/2017, איסור הפסקת טיפולים בשל הסכמים עם נותני שירותים).

### Step 4: Types of Treatment Available

Israeli mental health services offer several evidence-based treatment modalities.

| Treatment | Hebrew Name | Description | Best For |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|----------|
| CBT (Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) | טיפול קוגניטיבי-התנהגותי | Structured, goal-oriented therapy focusing on thought patterns | Anxiety, depression, OCD, phobias |
| EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) | EMDR | Trauma processing through guided eye movements | PTSD, trauma, distressing memories |
| Psychodynamic therapy | טיפול פסיכודינמי | Explores unconscious patterns and early experiences | Long-term personality issues, recurring relationship problems |
| Psychiatric medication | טיפול תרופתי | Prescribed by a psychiatrist (psych'iater, פסיכיאטר) | Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia |
| Group therapy | טיפול קבוצתי | Facilitated group sessions with shared experiences | Social anxiety, grief, addiction, support |
| Play therapy | טיפול במשחק | For children, using play as therapeutic medium | Children ages 3-12 with emotional difficulties |

Post-October 7 expansion (still active 2025-2026):
- EMDR services were significantly expanded across all kupot cholim
- Additional trauma-focused therapists were trained and deployed
- Group therapy programs for communities affected by the events were established
- Resilience centers (merkazei chosen, מרכזי חוסן) operated by the Ministry of Health in the south, north, Judea and Samaria, plus a national resilience center created after October 7. Together they serve roughly 1.3 million residents (per State Comptroller 2025 report)
- Sal briut 2026 added two psychiatric medications for adults with major depression: Auvelity (rapid-acting NMDA-targeting agent) and Riagility (dopamine-receptor agent helpful for negative symptoms). Always verify current sal briut coverage with your kupat cholim pharmacist.

### Step 5: Private Therapy Options and Costs

For those who prefer private therapy or cannot wait for kupat cholim placement. The 2025 HebPsy rate survey put the national average at around 395 NIS per session (about 2% higher than 2024), with the Tel Aviv / Shfela area roughly 40 NIS above the periphery.

| Option | Cost Range (per session, 2025-2026) | Notes |
|--------|------------------------|-------|
| Private psychologist (psikholog, פסיכולוג) | HebPsy 2025 survey average 421 NIS per session | Licensed clinical psychologist |
| Private psychiatrist (psikh'iater, פסיכיאטר) | Not covered by the HebPsy survey. Ask the practice directly | Can prescribe medication |
| Private social worker (oveid sotsiali, עובד סוציאלי) | HebPsy 2025 survey average 371 NIS per session | Licensed clinical social worker, often more available |
| Online therapy platforms | Not covered by the HebPsy survey. Varies widely by platform | English and Hebrew options available |
| Art therapy (tipul be'omanut, טיפול באומנות) | HebPsy 2025 survey average 366 NIS per session | Expressive arts therapist |

Finding a private therapist:
- BeTipulNet (בטיפולנט, betipulnet.co.il): largest Israeli therapist directory, searchable by specialty, location, and therapy type
- HebPsy (hebpsy.net): Hebrew-language professional portal with a therapist directory and a kupat cholim therapist agreement (hesder) listing
- Get Help Israel (gethelpisrael.com): English-language directory of verified mental health professionals
- Israel Psychological Association referral service
- Word of mouth and physician recommendations
- University training clinics offer reduced rates. Ask the specific clinic for its current rate

Tax benefits:
- Mental health expenses may be partially tax-deductible if prescribed as medical treatment. Confirm the applicable provision with a tax adviser or the Tax Authority rather than relying on a section number quoted here
- Keep all receipts (kabbalot, קבלות) for tax filing

### Step 6: Workplace Mental Health

Israeli labor law provides protections for employees dealing with mental health challenges.

| Right | Details |
|-------|---------|
| Sick days (yemei machala, ימי מחלה) | Mental health conditions qualify for sick leave, same as physical illness |
| Sick day accrual | 1.5 days per month of employment (18 per year), capped at 90 accumulated days under the Sick Pay Law (chok dmei machala) |
| First day payment | Unpaid; days 2-3 at 50%; day 4+ at 100% of salary |
| Confidentiality | Employer receives a sick note (ishur machala, אישור מחלה) without diagnosis details |
| Discrimination protection | Illegal to fire or discriminate based on mental health condition (Equal Rights for People with Disabilities Law, 1998) |
| Workplace accommodations | Employer may be required to provide reasonable accommodations |

Employee assistance programs (EAP):
- Many Israeli employers offer EAP services (tochnit siyua le'ovdim, תוכנית סיוע לעובדים)
- Typically provides 3-6 free confidential therapy sessions
- Accessible through HR department
- Covers personal, family, and work-related issues

### Step 7: PTSD-Specific Resources

PTSD (post-traumatic stress disorder, הפרעת דחק פוסט-טראומטית) is widespread in Israel. The Ministry of Defense Rehabilitation Division reported in late 2025 that it is handling around 82,400 wounded, of whom about 31,000 are dealing with PTSD or other mental health conditions, with around 1,500 new recognition requests per month and an expected additional 10,000 recognitions by end of 2026.

| Resource | Description | Contact |
|----------|-------------|---------|
| Natal | National trauma center, specialized PTSD treatment | *3362 |
| Nefesh Achat | 24/7 post-trauma support line, MoD Rehabilitation with Natal | *8944 |
| BeYadayim Tovot | Free help filing an IDF-disability claim and preparing for medical committees | *3757 |
| Bituach Leumi general disability claim | Form 7801 for mental health disability recognition | btl.gov.il or local branch |
| Bituach Leumi hostility victims (nifgaei eiva, נפגעי איבה) | Recognition path for civilian terror and war victims | btl.gov.il, Form 571 for Iron Swords claims |
| Resilience centers (merkazei chosen, מרכזי חוסן) | Community-based trauma support, MoH-operated | Located in municipalities, mostly south, north, and Judea and Samaria |
| MoD Rehabilitation, Iron Swords | Information desk for wounded reservists and soldiers from Oct 2023 onward | *6500 |
| MoD Rehabilitation, general | Recognition, medical committees, clinic and welfare services | *8150 |
| OneFamily Fund | Support for terror victims and families | 1-800-800-195 |

Bituach Leumi general disability recognition (mental health track):
1. Complete Form 7801 (tviya le'kitzbat nechut klalit, תביעה לקצבת נכות כללית) online or at a local branch
2. Attach a psychiatric or psychological opinion plus relevant medical documentation
3. Attend a medical committee evaluation (vaada refuit, ועדה רפואית)
4. Eligibility requires medical disability of at least 60%, or at least 40% if there are multiple impairments and one of them reaches 25%
5. As of 01.01.2026 Bituach Leumi publishes a full monthly pension of 4,711 NIS at a full incapacity grade (100% or 75%), 3,211 NIS at 74%, 2,894 NIS at 65% and 2,718 NIS at 60%, plus 1,214 NIS per child and 1,518 NIS for a spouse at the full grade (spouse income ceiling 7,848 NIS gross per month). Eligibility is also income-tested; the current earnings threshold is published on the Bituach Leumi eligibility page and should be read there rather than quoted from memory
6. The mental health rehabilitation basket (sal shikum, סל שיקום) is a SEPARATE Ministry of Health entitlement at a lower bar. It does not require a pension or an earning-capacity finding. Israeli residents aged 18 and over with at least 40% psychiatric disability qualify, determined either by Bituach Leumi or by a psychiatrist authorized by the Ministry of Health. Application is to a regional rehabilitation committee. Covered services include housing (hostels, supported and assisted living), employment, education, dental treatment, leisure, therapy coordination, initial-equipment funding, social mentorship, and family counseling centers. Your outpatient clinic is obliged to help you prepare the committee application; that assistance is itself part of the health basket.

For terror or war-related trauma, the hostility-victims track at Bituach Leumi (medical/financial benefits via the Ministry of Defense determining authority) is usually faster and more generous than the general disability track. Use Form 571 for Iron Swords claims.

Post-October 7 programs (still active 2025-2026):
- Extended PTSD treatment coverage through all kupot cholim
- Emergency mental health response teams deployed to affected communities
- EMDR training programs expanded for therapists nationwide
- Digital mental health platforms made available at no cost
- Reservists who served 30+ days under tzav 8 (order 8) since January 1, 2025 are eligible for reimbursed psychological, emotional, and couples therapy for themselves and for their spouses and children up to age 14
- Late 2025 policy clarification by Bituach Leumi recognizes that PTSD is not necessarily a time-limited condition, and additional treatment series for recognized hostility victims are approved with greater flexibility

## Examples

### Example 1: Starting Therapy for Anxiety
User says: "I've been having panic attacks and want to start therapy. How do I do this through my kupat cholim?"

Actions:
1. Call your kupat cholim's mental health department (machleket briut hanefesh)
2. Request an intake appointment; describe your symptoms (panic attacks, anxiety)
3. You will be assigned to a therapist, likely a CBT practitioner for anxiety
4. Get a referral from your family doctor first, and ask whether your kupah also needs Form 17 or a pre-authorization request
5. Cost depends on the setting. At a Ministry of Health public clinic or an agreement clinic there is typically no charge; at a public-hospital outpatient clinic Maccabi publishes 41 NIS per quarter. Check your own kupah's copay booklet
6. If wait times are long, ask for a referral to a private clinic UNDER AGREEMENT with your kupah, where the course of treatment is set by the therapist with no session cap. Do not expect reimbursement for a therapist you find yourself

Result: Patient begins weekly CBT sessions through their kupat cholim. Sessions are subsidized under the 2015 mental health reform. If the assigned therapist is not a good match, the patient can request reassignment.

### Example 2: Crisis Support After a Difficult Event
User says: "My friend just told me they're having suicidal thoughts. What do I do right now?"

Actions:
1. Stay with your friend; do not leave them alone
2. Call ERAN at 1201 together, or call on their behalf
3. If there is immediate danger, call 101 (MDA) or 100 (police)
4. Encourage your friend to go to the nearest hospital ER psychiatric emergency (cheder miyun psikh'iatri)
5. After the immediate crisis, help them schedule an appointment with their kupat cholim

Result: ERAN provides immediate crisis intervention and guidance. If hospitalization is needed, the ER psychiatric team evaluates and can arrange voluntary or involuntary admission for safety.

### Example 3: Navigating Workplace Mental Health Leave
User says: "I need to take time off work for depression but I'm worried about my job. What are my rights?"

Actions:
1. Visit your psychiatrist or family doctor to obtain a sick note (ishur machala)
2. The sick note does not need to specify "depression" or any diagnosis
3. Submit the sick note to your employer's HR department
4. You are entitled to 1.5 sick days per month (18 per year)
5. Your employer cannot fire you during sick leave or discriminate based on your condition
6. If you need extended leave, discuss with your doctor about ishur machala for a longer period

Result: Employee takes mental health sick leave. Employer receives a generic sick note without diagnosis. Employment is protected under Israeli labor law and the Equal Rights for People with Disabilities Law.

### Example 4: Seeking PTSD Treatment
User says: "I've been having flashbacks and nightmares since a traumatic event. Where do I get help?"

Actions:
1. For immediate support, call Natal at *3362 (specialized trauma hotline)
2. Contact your kupat cholim to request a referral for PTSD-specific treatment
3. Ask specifically for EMDR or trauma-focused CBT, both proven effective for PTSD
4. If symptoms are severe, request an urgent psychiatric evaluation
5. If you are an IDF disabled person, a discharged soldier, or a family member, the Nefesh Achat line *8944 is available 24/7 for post-trauma support
6. If the trauma is related to a recognized event (military service, terror attack, war), file with the appropriate track: Form 571 for nifgaei eiva (Iron Swords hostility-victim claim), Form 7801 for general mental health disability, or contact MoD Rehabilitation at *8150 (Iron Swords casualties: *6500). Free help preparing an IDF-disability claim is available from BeYadayim Tovot at *3757

Result: Patient is connected to trauma-specialized treatment through their kupat cholim. EMDR availability has been significantly expanded. Recognition, if applicable, provides additional financial support and treatment access.

## Gotchas

- The 2015 mental health reform transferred psychiatric services from government to kupot cholim. Agents may still reference the old government-run system or suggest outdated access paths.
- ERAN's number is 1201, not a standard 4-digit or toll-free format. Agents may generate plausible but incorrect crisis line numbers. Always verify hotline numbers against the reference data.
- Natal's phone number changed from the legacy 1-800-363-363 to *3362. Agents trained on older data will use the old number. Always use *3362.
- The kupah session entitlement is a two-stage structure (2 assessment sessions plus a 15-session kartisiya within one year), not a vague "12-16 per year". At a private clinic under agreement there is no session cap at all. Agents commonly state one number for both settings.
- Copay for a psychiatrist is set by SETTING, not by kupah. There is no single national number. Agents commonly invent one and apply it to all four kupot.
- Sal shikum requires 40% psychiatric disability at age 18+, and is separate from the general disability pension (which needs 60% medical disability). Agents commonly merge the two thresholds.
- Israeli sick days for mental health follow the same payment structure as physical illness: day 1 unpaid, days 2-3 at 50%, day 4+ at 100%. Agents may apply US-style sick leave policies instead.
- Post-October 7 mental health services were significantly expanded, including EMDR availability and streamlined PTSD recognition. Agents trained before late 2023 will not reflect these expansions.
- MoD Rehabilitation runs three distinct numbers and agents mix them up: *8150 is the general call center, *6500 is the Iron Swords casualties desk, and *8944 (Nefesh Achat) is the only one of the three that is a 24/7 emotional-support line. *3104 is not an MoD Rehabilitation number; if you see it, it is wrong. If in doubt, route the user to shikum.mod.gov.il.
- The national sexual assault lines are 1202 (answered by women) and 1203 (answered by men). 1-800-220-000 is the family violence line, a different service. Agents frequently swap them.
- Bituach Leumi forms have specific numbers: Form 7801 for general disability (kitzbat nechut klalit), Form 571 for Iron Swords hostility-victim claims. Agents often hallucinate form numbers, so cite a btl.gov.il URL instead of guessing.

## Troubleshooting

### Error: "My kupat cholim says there's a 2-month wait for therapy"
Cause: High demand for mental health services, especially since 2023, has created long wait times in many kupot cholim.
Solution: Ask for a referral to a private clinic UNDER AGREEMENT with your kupah (מרפאה פרטית שבהסכם). This is the official alternative route, it costs nothing, and the course of treatment is set by the therapist rather than capped. Do not ask for reimbursement for a therapist you found yourself; Maccabi, for example, states plainly that no reimbursement is given for private mental health services. You can also check whether supplementary insurance (shaban) provides faster access. In the meantime, call ERAN (1201) for immediate support.

### Error: "I was told therapy through kupat cholim is only for severe conditions"
Cause: Some kupot cholim intake workers may incorrectly gatekeep access, prioritizing severe cases. However, the 2015 reform guarantees coverage for all mental health needs, not just severe psychiatric conditions.
Solution: Insist on your right to treatment under the National Health Insurance Law. If refused, file a complaint with the kupat cholim's patient ombudsman (netziv kvilot hatzibur, נציב קבילות הציבור). You can also contact the Ministry of Health mental health division at *5400 for guidance.

### Error: "My employer asked me why I'm taking sick days"
Cause: Some employers improperly request medical details beyond what is legally required. An employer is entitled to receive a sick note confirming the employee cannot work, but not the diagnosis or reason.
Solution: Provide only the standard sick note (ishur machala) from your doctor. Politely inform HR that you are not required to disclose your diagnosis. If pressured, contact the Ministry of Labor public-inquiries service at gov.il, or the Kav LaOved organization at kavlaoved.org.il, for guidance on your rights.
