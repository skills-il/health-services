# Elder Housing Options in Israel (2026)

## Overview of Options

| Option | Independence Level | Cost structure | Entry cost |
|--------|-------------------|----------------|------------|
| Living at home with care | Varies | Caregiver salary, partly offset by gimlat siud | None |
| Retirement home (diur mugan) | High | Monthly management fee | Large, partly refundable deposit |
| Assisted living | Medium | Monthly fee, above a retirement home | Varies |
| Nursing home (beit avot siudi) | Low | Monthly fee | None typically |
| Dementia ward | Low | Monthly fee, the highest of these | None typically |

This skill deliberately does not quote price ranges. They move, they vary sharply between
the centre and the periphery, and a stale range anchors a family to the wrong budget. Get
current figures from the specific facility, and use the Ministry of Health licensed-institution
list to shortlist.

## Living at Home with Care

### Government support (gimlat siud)
- 5.5 to 30 hours/week of home care based on ADL level (6 levels)
- Free from Bituach Leumi for eligible elderly

### Foreign caregiver (metapel/et zarim)
- Live-in arrangement typical
- Salary (April 2026): minimum 6,443.85 NIS/month gross for 24/6 (257.75 NIS/day, 35.40 NIS/hour). On top of the gross salary budget for social benefits, pension and severance reserves, plus the full-post top-up below
- Room and board provided in kind (not in cash)
- Requires work permit (heter ha'asaka) from the Population and Immigration Authority (PIBA)
- Gimlat siud hours reduced at Levels 3-6 when employing a foreign worker (see Level table)
- Cash benefit option amounts are lower when employing a foreign worker
- Permit criteria (community-dwelling, past retirement age): at least 4.5 dependency points,
  OR at least 4 points plus the living-alone (bodedim) bonus, OR at least 0.5 point in the
  supervision item plus the bodedim bonus. Age 85+ who misses those goes to an advisory
  committee on medical documents; age 90+ needs 4 points.
- Someone whose INCOME disqualifies them from gimlat siud can still take the ADL test purely
  to establish the foreign-worker permit. Do not tell such a family they have no options.
- The permit is available to residents of diur mugan and to day-hospitalisation patients.
- When the caregiver comes through a siud company, the family must top the post up to a full
  week (42 weekly / 182 monthly hours) at its own expense.

### Private home care agency
- Hourly caregivers for part-time needs
- Charged per hour, with the rate depending on the number of hours and the agency

## Retirement Homes (Diur Mugan)

### What they offer
- Independent apartment within a managed community
- Shared dining, social activities, medical clinic on-site
- Emergency call system
- Maintenance and security
- Option to receive additional care services as needs increase

### Financial model
- Large upfront deposit (400K-1.8M NIS) -- partially or fully refundable
- Monthly management fee, charged on top of the deposit
- Central Israel more expensive than periphery
- Some operate on rental model without deposit

### Who it's for
- Independent elderly who want community and security
- Couples where one may need more care in the future
- Those who want guaranteed care escalation on-site

## Nursing Homes (Beit Avot Siudi)

### Types of wings
- Frail elderly (tashush guf) -- needs partial physical assistance
- Full nursing (siudi maleh) -- needs constant nursing care
- Mentally frail (tashush nefesh) -- cognitive decline, dementia
- Ventilator-dependent -- intensive medical care

### Costs
- Private: paid in full by the family
- Government-subsidized: through Ministry of Health "code" (tzofan)
- Semi-private: some government subsidy plus family co-payment

### Government subsidized placement ("code" / tzofan)
- Apply to the district health office (lishkat habriut). Local social services (sherut revaha)
  handle the "frail" (tashush) track through the Ministry of Welfare.
- A nurse or social worker visits within 14 working days of a complete file. A classification
  committee (vaadat siyug) then decides "siudi", "tashush nefesh", or another category.
- **The co-payment is means-tested on the elder, their spouse, AND their adult children.**
  Each child files a sworn declaration questionnaire signed before a lawyer or court clerk,
  disclosing income, savings, deposits and any private siudi policy. Warn families before
  they apply.
- Appeal the classification in writing to the committee, then to the Head of the Geriatrics
  Division at the Ministry of Health (*5400).
- A classification expires if the elder has not been admitted within 3 months.
- Verify the facility holds a current Ministry of Health licence before choosing it.
- Waiting lists can be significant in central Israel

### What to look for when choosing
- Government licensing status (check with Ministry of Health)
- Staff-to-resident ratio
- Medical staff qualifications and availability
- Cleanliness and maintenance
- Activity programming
- Location relative to family
- Specific experience with the elder's condition (dementia, physical disability)
