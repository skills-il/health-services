# Ministry of Education standing instruction 0042

**Title:** התנהלות מוסדות החינוך והצהרונים לשם הבטחת בריאותם של תלמידים הסובלים מאלרגיות למוצרי מזון
**Instruction:** הוראת קבע מספר 0042, החלפה
**In force from:** 21.11.2017
**Published in:** חוזר מנכ"ל תשע"ח/4, ג' בכסלו תשע"ח
**Applies to:** כלל מוסדות החינוך והצהרונים בכל המגזרים

Note on numbering: this is often referred to loosely as "circular 4/2018". The
circular number is the Hebrew-year form תשע"ח/4, and the standing instruction
itself is numbered 0042. Use the instruction number when asking a school
secretary to locate it.

## Institution obligations

**Zone definition, by allergen behaviour.**

Airborne-dispersal allergen (peanuts, nuts in natural form, snacks containing
peanuts): define the educational institution or the activity framework as a
zone clean of the allergen, and note prominently within the institution, in the
activity area and outside it, with signage visible to all, that the allergen and
products containing it may not be brought in.

Non-airborne allergen, where reaction is caused by contact or ingestion: the
classroom, the activity area and further spaces agreed upon are declared a safe
environment into which the allergen may not be brought, announced by signs hung
in those places. The signage must be prominent and identifiable during all
activity hours.

**Daily routine.** Timetable arranged so most lessons are in the home classroom
and, as far as possible, eating takes place after the long break. When learning
in other spaces, prepare in advance, for example by assigning a duty student to
wipe the allergic student's desk with a wipe before the lesson. Where several
foods are involved, plan so institutional routine is disrupted as little as
possible while safety is preserved. Avoid merging tzaharonim before the meal is
served; merge only afterwards.

**Awareness.** The principal of the school, gan or tzaharon, or their delegate,
must act to raise awareness of food allergy among the educational team, the
staff, the parents and the students, and implement the circular throughout the
school day and in every educational activity, formal and informal.

**Emotional and social support.** Arrange accompaniment of the student by the
institution's counsellor or psychologist. The tzaharon manager must receive
guidance from the psychologist or counsellor on the emotional support required.
Avoid discrimination or exclusion on grounds of the allergy. Plan menus in
advance for holidays (such as mishloach manot), health meals, food sales and
trips so the allergen is excluded, enlisting the parents' leadership and the
community. Allow the student to present the nature of their own allergy at the
start of the year or the tzaharon activity, according to age and willingness and
with parental involvement.

## Parent obligations

1. Sign a health declaration (הצהרת בריאות) every year, per the standing
   instruction on parents' declaration of their child's health, attaching a
   medical document from the treating allergist describing the allergy. The
   document details the foods and substances the child is allergic to and
   clarifies the actions to take on exposure.
2. Report at tzaharon registration that the child has a food allergy.
3. Deposit an in-date injector in the emergency medicine box at the school, gan
   or tzaharon facility, marked with the student's name and details plus a
   photograph of the HMO card. A second injector bearing the name stays in the
   student's personal bag. Replace each injector when it expires.
4. Sign the forms permitting medication administration, per section 2.2-74 of
   standing instruction עב/1(ב), "מתן טיפול תרופתי במוסד החינוך".
5. Before a trip, out-of-school activity or a tzaharon serving food, request the
   catering provider's contact details and approve the composition of the food.
   If not approved, the parents must be allowed to supply suitable food.

## Medical assistant (סייעת רפואית)

Life-threatening food allergy is included in the criteria allowing focused
assistance from the inter-ministerial committee for employing a personal escort
(medical assistant) in mainstream education, per section 19-2.2 of standing
instruction עז/4(א), "סייעות רפואיות בחינוך הרגיל".

Two limits stated in the same passage, and the most frequently violated part of
the whole instruction:

- Parents may not be compelled to submit an application for assistance via the
  local authority.
- A student may not be prevented from taking part in an educational activity at
  or on behalf of the institution in the absence of an assistant, even where the
  student has been found eligible.

## Clinical background stated in the instruction

- Reaction mechanism: over-reaction of the immune system to protein components
  in food via IgE antibodies. Reactions develop from eating the allergenic food
  even in a minimal quantity; in rare cases contact alone causes a significant
  reaction.
- Severity range: skin and mucous membranes, digestive system, respiratory
  system, cardiovascular system, up to anaphylactic shock involving all systems.
- Onset: in severe cases, within a few minutes to two hours of exposure, airway
  swelling and edema with severe shortness of breath requiring immediate
  adrenaline injection to save life.
- Epidemiology: about 5% of children. Main allergens in Israel are milk protein,
  peanuts, nuts, sesame, eggs and fish. Milk and egg allergy usually resolves by
  age three, but 10% to 15% of milk-allergic children continue past that age.
  Peanut, nut and fish allergy persists long and sometimes appears first at
  school age.
- A first reaction may occur at the institution in a student with no previous
  allergic history, which demands heightened alertness.
