# Bituach Leumi disabled-child claim for food allergy

Source of every criterion below: the Bituach Leumi allergy criterion page,
https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Disabled_Child/likuilist/Pages/allergy.aspx

## The gate, in the order to check it

Check age first. It disqualifies more families than everything else combined.

| Element | Criterion, as published |
|---|---|
| Age window | From age 9 months to age 10 years |
| Rate | 50%, amounting to 1,943 NIS |
| Diagnostic confirmation | Allergy test criteria: skin prick test (SPT), blood test, or challenge test (מבחן תגר) |
| Allergist recommendation | Recommendation from a physician specialising in allergy to be permanently equipped with an EpiPen, or a reasoned justification for refraining from carrying one |
| Episode documentation | Real-time medical documentation of the treatment given during an attack that included a respiratory component or anaphylactic shock |

The rate updates every January along with the rest of the Bituach Leumi
schedule. Always restate the effective year and send the family to the source
before they budget on the figure.

## Why the third element is the one that fails

The first two are static. A family that has an allergist has the test results
and can request the EpiPen recommendation letter at any routine appointment.

The third cannot be produced on demand. It has to be captured at the moment of
an actual reaction, in the emergency room or clinic, at the visit itself. A
family that has had two frightening anaphylactic episodes and never collected
the paperwork has no admissible evidence of either.

**The forward-looking action.** At the first conversation with any newly
diagnosed family, tell them: if the child is ever treated for a reaction
involving breathing difficulty or anaphylaxis, ask for the treatment
documentation before leaving. Keep it. It costs nothing at the time and it is
the difference between a claim that succeeds and one that does not.

## Note on the rate

50% is the rate published for the allergy criterion specifically. The
disabled-child allowance carries different rates under different criteria, and
the sibling `israeli-oncology-navigator` skill documents a four-rate structure
for other conditions. Never carry a rate across from one criterion to another.

## If the child is over 10

Say so directly. A claim on the allergy criterion will not succeed. Two things
remain available and are worth redirecting to:

1. The Ministry of Education accommodations, which carry no age limit at all.
2. The general disabled-child route, if the child has a separate qualifying
   condition. That runs on its own criterion and belongs with the general
   Bituach Leumi claim process rather than this skill.
