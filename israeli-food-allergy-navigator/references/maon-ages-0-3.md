# Ages 0-3: maon (daycare) and mishpachton

Verified 2026-10-12. Instruction 0042 does not reach this age group: its scope line
is "כלל מוסדות החינוך והצהרונים בכל המגזרים" and its body speaks of school, gan and
tzaharon. A maon is governed by a different set of texts, below.

## Who is responsible

- **Supervision and licensing: Ministry of Education**, under the Daycare
  Supervision Law for Toddlers, 5779-2018 (חוק הפיקוח על מעונות יום לפעוטות,
  התשע"ט-2018). Standing instruction 0336 describes the Ministry's role "כמיישם חוק
  הפיקוח על מעונות יום לפעוטות".
  https://apps.education.gov.il/Mankal/Horaa.aspx?siduri=436
- **Allergy aide: apply through the Ministry of Labor, funded by the Welfare
  Ministry.** The Welfare Director-General circular on aides for toddlers with
  life-threatening allergy states the service is given at "100% מימון משרד הרווחה
  והביטחון החברתי" (section 2.6), with applications received by the Ministry of
  Labor's parent-employment division; toddlers at risk are routed through the
  local social-services department (section 2.4). The circular for
  2025-26 states "השירות יינתן מתאריך 1.9.2025 עד לתאריך 31.8.2026" and that it is
  not in force beyond that date. No successor had been published when this was
  checked (October 2026). **Do not state the 2026-27 terms.** Send the family to the
  gov.il service page: https://www.gov.il/he/service/assistance-for-toddler-with-severe-allergy
  (it may block automated access; it opens normally in a browser).

## What binds a licensed maon

Source: the Ministry of Education daycare operating booklet, "אוגדן הנחיות להפעלת
מעון יום לפעוטות", version 1, April 2022, section 8,
https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/daycare-operating-instructions-booklet.pdf

- **No refusal, no removal (8.1):** "חל איסור להפלות פעוט הסובל מאלרגיה בכל הקשור
  לקבלתו למעון. פעוט הסובל מאלרגיה יוכל להתקבל למעון ולא יורחק ממנו במהלך שנת
  לימודים", subject to the parent duties below.
- **Parent duties (8.1):** a medical questionnaire at registration, a document from
  an allergist, a written undertaking to supply the medication at the parent's own
  cost and renew it by expiry date, and a written authorisation for staff to give
  the treatment in the document. The booklet also asks the parent to attend a
  parents' meeting and staff briefing, and to sign a medical-confidentiality
  waiver so other parents can be enlisted. **That confidentiality waiver is not
  the same thing as the liability waiver 0042 forbids in schools**; it is a
  maon-specific requirement and should be described accurately.
- **After treatment (8.1(6)):** "בכל מקרה של מתן טיפול תרופתי מניעתי של טיפות אנטי
  היסטמיניות או מזרק אפינפרין במעון, יש לפנות את הפעוט לביה"ח באמבולנס. במקביל יש
  לידע טלפונית באופן מיידי את ההורים."
- **No exclusion, no separation (8.3(2)):** "חל איסור להדיר פעוט עם אלרגיה מכל
  פעילות שהיא וחל איסור להפריד אותו מהקבוצה שלו".
- **Signage (8.3(3)):** "חובה להציב שילוט במקומות המתאימים, האוסר הכנסת מוצרים
  אלרגניים לשטח המעון."
- **Training (8.2(3)):** mandatory: "כל הצוות במעון מחוייב לעבור קורס עזרה ראשונה
  הכולל את תכני ההכשרה בנושא אלרגיה". Optional on top: a specific allergy training
  coordinated with MDA ("קיימת אפשרות לתאם הדרכה ספציפית בנושא אלרגיה, מול ארגון
  מד"א"), held at MDA sites, which covers injector use. Do not present the MDA
  training as a duty.
- **Posted plan (8.4):** the response procedure must appear "במקום בולט בכתת
  הפעוט הרלוונטי", and staff must know where the toddlers' own injectors and
  antihistamines are kept.
- **Medication (section 7(2)):** life-saving medication may be given by staff on a
  written specialist recommendation for that specific toddler plus the parent's
  written approval.

## The epinephrine stock duty

The Epinephrine in Educational Institutions and Public Places Law, 5778-2018
(חוק החזקת תכשיר אפינפרין במוסדות חינוך ובמקומות ציבוריים, התשע"ח-2018) extends to a
maon intended for the daily stay of **at least ten toddlers** under age three
("עשרה פעוטות"). A smaller setting, a mishpachton or an unlicensed home gan is not
reached through that route. Source: the law as published,
https://fs.knesset.gov.il/20/law/20_lsr_504201.pdf

## What to tell the family

1. Ask whether the setting is a licensed maon and how many toddlers it holds.
2. Bring the allergist document, the medication, and the written authorisation.
3. Ask for the signage and for confirmation the allergen is removed from the
   toddler's group, citing section 8 of the booklet.
4. Ask for confirmation that all staff completed the mandatory first-aid course
   with its allergy content, whether the optional MDA allergy training was
   arranged, and that the plan is posted in the toddler's room.
5. For the meal-time aide, apply through the gov.il service and check the current
   year's terms there.
