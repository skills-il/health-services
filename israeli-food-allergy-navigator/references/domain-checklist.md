# Domain Coverage Checklist, israeli-food-allergy-navigator

Generated: 2026-07-25, via Ministry of Education standing instruction 0042 (full
PDF text), Bituach Leumi allergy criterion page, Ministry of Health allergen
guidance, and the Israeli food allergy association.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] Standing instruction identity, הוראת קבע 0042, in force 21.11.2017, published in חוזר מנכ"ל תשע"ח/4, source: allergyisrael.org.il circular PDF, why core: every accommodation right derives from it and the number is what unlocks it at a school office
- [x] Zone definition BY ALLERGEN BEHAVIOUR, the two-branch rule, source: circular section 2, why core: the obligation differs categorically. Airborne (peanut, nut in natural form) obliges the WHOLE institution to be allergen-clean; contact/ingestion obliges only classroom plus agreed spaces. Getting this backwards either under-claims or over-claims the right
- [x] Signage requirement, prominent, visible to all, identifiable during ALL activity hours, source: circular section 2, why core: the enforceable, checkable artifact
- [x] Parent packet, all four components, source: circular section 4.1 to 4.4, why core: annual health declaration, allergist document, TWO in-date injectors with HMO card copy, medication forms per עב/1(ב) section 2.2-74. Families routinely deposit only one injector
- [x] Tzaharon registration disclosure as a SEPARATE obligation, source: circular section 4.1, why core: happens months before the school year and is forgotten
- [x] Medical assistant criterion, עז/4(א) section 19-2.2, source: circular, why core: names the eligibility route
- [x] The two medical-assistant LIMITS, source: circular, why core: parents may not be compelled to apply via the local authority, and a child may not be excluded from activity for want of an assistant even when eligible. Most violated provision in the instruction
- [x] Bituach Leumi allergy criterion, ALL FIVE sub-dimensions enumerated, source: btl.gov.il allergy.aspx, why core: age window 9 months to 10 years; rate 50% = 1,943 NIS; tests SPT/blood/challenge; allergist EpiPen recommendation OR reasoned justification for refraining; real-time documentation of a respiratory or anaphylactic episode. A row saying "covers the allowance" would pass while omitting the age gate that disqualifies most claimants
- [x] Real-time documentation as a FORWARD-LOOKING action, source: btl.gov.il allergy.aspx, why core: it cannot be reconstructed retroactively, so the advice must be given before the episode, not after
- [x] Anaphylaxis onset window and response, source: circular section 1, why core: minutes to two hours; adrenaline is first line; 101
- [x] Institution obligations beyond zoning, source: circular sections 2 and 3, why core: timetable, desk wiping, tzaharon merge timing, menu pre-planning for holidays and trips, emotional support, non-discrimination, class-parent notification, catering approval
- [x] Trips and catering, parents' right to approve composition or supply food, source: circular section 4.4, why core: the recurring flashpoint

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] Epidemiology and allergen list for Israel, source: circular section 1, why: milk protein, peanuts, nuts, sesame, eggs, fish; about 5%; resolution patterns
- [x] First reaction may occur at the institution with no prior history, source: circular section 1, why: shapes staff-alertness framing
- [x] OIT status, source: allergysite.co.il, why: most-asked question. Not in the health basket, private, roughly 3 to 4 year wait, registration from age 3, treatment from age 4. Confidence medium, re-verify each cycle
- [x] Allergen labelling and the 2025 food-reform change, source: gov.il allergens-guidance and gov.il 02012025-01, why: the main adult-facing protection
- [x] Over-10 dead end, handled explicitly rather than by omission
- [ ] Named per-municipality סייעת application windows, deliberately NOT stated, they vary by authority and year, the skill instructs the user to check their own

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- Celiac and gluten, different mechanism, no EpiPen, no anaphylaxis pathway, different entitlements, related skill: `israeli-celiac-navigator`
- Meal planning, menu building and general nutrition labelling, related skill: `israeli-nutrition-planner`
- Clinical diagnosis, allergy testing interpretation, drug dosing, medical scope, always route to an allergist
- General Bituach Leumi benefits beyond the allergy criterion, related skill: `israeli-bituach-leumi`
- Gan and school registration, subsidies, special-education placement, related skill: `israeli-childcare-navigator`
- Emergency medical services generally, related skill: `israeli-emergency-guide`
- Non-Israeli allergy systems
