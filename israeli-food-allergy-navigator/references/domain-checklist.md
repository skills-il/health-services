## Must cover (core)

- [x] Standing instruction identity, הוראת קבע 0042, in force 21.11.2017, published in חוזר מנכ"ל תשע"ח/4, source: the live Mankal page siduri=51 (the allergyisrael.org.il PDF used earlier is now dead), why core: every accommodation right derives from it and the number is what unlocks it at a school office
- [x] Zone definition BY ALLERGEN BEHAVIOUR, the two-branch rule, source: circular section 2, why core: the obligation differs categorically. Airborne (peanut, nut in natural form) obliges the WHOLE institution to be allergen-clean; contact/ingestion obliges only classroom plus agreed spaces. Getting this backwards either under-claims or over-claims the right
- [x] Signage requirement, prominent, visible to all, identifiable during ALL activity hours, source: circular section 2, why core: the enforceable, checkable artifact
- [x] Parent packet, all four components, source: circular section 4.1 to 4.4, why core: annual health declaration, allergist document, TWO in-date injectors with HMO card copy, medication forms per עב/1(ב) section 2.2-74. Families routinely deposit only one injector
- [x] Tzaharon registration disclosure as a SEPARATE obligation, source: circular section 4.1, why core: happens months before the school year and is forgotten
- [x] Medical assistant criterion, עז/4(א) section 2.2-91, source: circular section 5.14 verbatim, why core: names the eligibility route. Corrected 2026-08-26 from "19-2.2", a digit-reversed rendering that appeared in five files including the ready-to-send letter template; the instruction text contains zero occurrences of 19-2.2
- [x] The FIVE prohibitions, counted as PROHIBITIONS not as sections, sources: circular sections 5.14 (which contains TWO), 5.15, 5.16, 6.14(ג). Why core: parents may not be compelled to apply via the local authority; a child may not be excluded from activity for want of an assistant even when eligible; attendance may not be conditioned on the allergy at all (5.15); parents may not be made to sign liability waivers (5.16); parents may not be compelled to escort a trip nor the trip conditioned on it (6.14(ג)). Sections 5.15, 5.16 and 6.14(ג) added 2026-08-26. **This row was itself written as "FOUR prohibitions" earlier in the same cycle that corrected the four-versus-five qualifying alternatives, by counting sections instead of prohibitions, and 5.15 fell out. That is the second instance of one failure mode in one skill: an enumeration labelled by the wrong axis. Every checklist row that counts something must name the unit it counts.**
- [x] Emergency action-plan card, source: circular sections 5.12, 5.13, 6.13, why core: it is the highest-risk artifact the skill emits and the one that physically sits in the emergency box. It must defer positioning (except the one RCUK-sourced rule added 2026-10-12: do not stand, walk or sit up suddenly until MDA decides) and repeat-dosing to the treating physician's instruction sheet, carry the 101 dispatcher handoff, carry the no-injector-available branch, and state that the child is never left alone. Added as a Must-cover row 2026-08-26 after an earlier version carried an unsourced, unqualified lie-flat instruction
- [x] Device strength versus expiry, why core: the skill mandates two in-date injectors and teaches expiry tracking. Auto-injectors are weight-banded, so a growing child can outgrow the junior device between prescriptions. The skill prompts an annual confirmation with the allergist and states NO strength, weight band or dose. Added 2026-08-26
- [x] Principal's preparation duties, source: circular section 6, why core: the pre-year meeting and named liaison (6.2), start-of-year training with HANDS-ON injector practice and briefing of substitutes (6.4, 6.12, 6.15), posting the protocol and family phone numbers in two places (6.9), vending machines (6.8), dietitian referral (6.5). Added 2026-08-26. The training duty is the one that decides whether an adult in the room can actually act
- [x] Exceptional-incident reporting, source: circular section 6.11, why core: an evacuation or injector use must be filed as an exceptional incident (tzaharon reports to the local authority or operator). Added 2026-08-26. This is a contemporaneous third-party record of exactly the event the BTL criterion turns on, so it is also claim evidence the family can request
- [x] Own food with proportional refund, source: circular section 5.8, why core: a monetary right, previously covered only as "or supply their own"
- [x] Bituach Leumi allergy criterion, GATE dimensions: age window 9 months to 10 years; rate 50% = 1,943 NIS effective 01.01.2026; required documents (tests SPT/blood/challenge, allergist EpiPen recommendation, real-time episode documentation with the active-asthma fallback). Source: btl.gov.il allergy.aspx
- [x] Bituach Leumi allergy criterion, the FIVE QUALIFYING ALTERNATIVES enumerated as alternatives, source: btl.gov.il allergy.aspx, why core: BTL's wording is "ועל אחד מהתנאים האלה" over five bullets: anaphylactic shock; anaphylactic reaction across two body systems; shortness of breath with oxygen-saturation drop; injected adrenaline; **and a standing allergist EpiPen recommendation, which requires no event at all**. Corrected 2026-08-26. The v1.0.0 checklist conflated this list with the evidence list above and recorded "ALL FIVE sub-dimensions" while naming five *documents*, so the row passed every gate while the skill shipped four alternatives and turned qualifying families away. A checklist row must name the axis it enumerates, or it certifies the wrong list
- [x] Real-time documentation as a FORWARD-LOOKING action, source: btl.gov.il allergy.aspx, why core: it cannot be reconstructed retroactively, so the advice must be given before the episode, not after
- [x] Anaphylaxis onset window and response, source: circular section 1, why core: minutes to two hours; adrenaline is first line; 101
- [x] Institution obligations beyond zoning, source: circular sections 2 and 3, why core: timetable, desk wiping, tzaharon merge timing, menu pre-planning for holidays and trips, emotional support, non-discrimination, class-parent notification, catering approval
- [x] Trips and catering, parents' right to approve composition or supply food, source: circular section 4.4, why core: the recurring flashpoint

- [x] CURRENT instruction numbers for the documents 0042 cross-refers to, added 2026-10-12. 0042 still cites 2.2-74 (medication) and 2.2-84 (health declaration), now superseded: 2.2-74 cancelled by 0384 on 29.12.2022, and 2.2-84 cancelled by 0083 on 4.2.2018, which 0382 replaced on 29.12.2022. - [x] The medical-assistant criterion AS WRITTEN in 0011 (the text behind "2.2-91 עז/4(א)"), added 2026-10-12: seven named allergens, "עם תיעוד ברור של חומרת התגובה ובשילוב של אסטמה אלרגית", a paediatric allergist document from the six months before applying, start age 3 in a municipal gan, independent of the BTL allowance, applied for and appealed only by the local authority, with a further ערר at the Ministry of Health. - [x] The institution's own general-use injector under the 2018 Epinephrine in Educational Institutions and Public Places Law (in force January 2019, local authority buys, "מזרק אפינפרין" sign, three-monthly expiry check), added 2026-10-12. - [x] Ages 0-3 (maon and mishpachton), added 2026-10-12, resolving the v1.1.0 [CARRY]. 0042 does not apply; the MoE daycare operating booklet section 8 does (no refusal or removal, no exclusion or separation, signage, ambulance after adrenaline or antihistamine, parents supply medication).
