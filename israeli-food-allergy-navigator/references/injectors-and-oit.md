# Injectors, the school stock injector, and OIT funding

Verified 2026-10-12. Every figure here comes from the source named next to it.
Nothing here is a dose recommendation.

## Health-basket status of each product

Source: the Ministry of Health drug registry (israeldrugs.health.gov.il, queried per
trade name through its API; the registry showed "מעודכן לתאריך: 12/10/2026").

| Product | Registered | In the basket |
|---|---|---|
| EpiPen and EpiPen Jr (אפיפן, אפיפן לילדים) | Yes | Yes, restricted to prescription by a specialist ("תרופה מוגבלת לרישום ע'י רופא מומחה") |
| Epinephrine Injection Mylan 0.3 mg and Mylan JR 0.15 mg (auto-injector) | Yes | Yes |
| Jext 0.15 mg and 0.3 mg (ג'קסט) | Yes, since 16.12.2025 | No |
| Teva epinephrine pre-filled syringes | Yes | No |
| neffy (nasal adrenaline), Anapen | Not registered | n/a |

Registration does not prove a product is stocked. **The registry carries no
per-year quantity, and no kupah page found states one.** Never state how many
injectors a kupah funds.

## The institution's own injector (a separate thing)

The 2018 epinephrine law obliges every educational institution to keep at least
one general-use injector. The Ministry of Education's summary
(https://meyda.education.gov.il/files/Bitachon/HealthEpinephrineinSchools.pdf):

- In force from January 2019; the local authority buys it, and may do so without a
  prescription.
- It sits in a central, accessible place, behind a sign reading "מזרק אפינפרין" at
  the entrance and on every floor, and its expiry is checked "אחת לשלושה חודשים
  לפחות".
- The sign's order of actions applies "בהיעדר הוראה רפואית אחרת": call 101, fetch
  the injector, follow the dispatcher.

This injector is **not a substitute** for the child's own two injectors under
0042 section 4.2. Its use is the no-injector-available branch: a parent can ask
the school to confirm where it is.

## OIT funding

- **Shamir (Assaf Harofeh):** the programme page states "הטיפול הינו טיפול פרטי,
  אינו כלול בסל הבריאות או ממומן ע"י הביטוחים המשלימים".
  https://www.shamir.org/he/clinic/foodallergyclinic/program/
- **The 2026 basket expansion** (Director-General circular published 8 March 2026)
  contains no food-allergy, OIT or epinephrine item; its only allergy items concern
  house-dust-mite immunotherapy. Checked against the circular's text as mirrored by
  the Israeli Association of Family Medicine.
- **Other centres:** funding not verified. Send the family to their kupah.
- Do not quote success rates as a promise, and never imply OIT removes the need
  for an injector.
