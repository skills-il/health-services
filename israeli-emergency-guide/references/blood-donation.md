# Blood donation through MDA

MDA Blood Services (שירותי הדם של מד"א) manages Israel's entire blood supply.

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| Who can donate | First donation between ages 18 and 60, weight over 50 kg, in good health. Ages 17-18 need parental or guardian consent. A first donation over 60, or a repeat donation over 65, needs the treating physician's approval renewed annually, must meet the donor metrics, and must take place at a fixed site staffed by someone certified in basic resuscitation. |
| How often | At least 3 months since the previous donation (whole blood) |
| Where to donate | MDA blood donation centers, mobile blood drives |
| Scheduling | MDA Blood Services on 03-5300400, or hatramot_dam@mda.org.il. **Never tell a user to call 101 to book a blood donation**. 101 is the ambulance dispatch line. |
| Time required | MDA does not publish a figure. Allow time for registration, screening and a rest period afterwards |

MDA Blood Services supplies Israel's hospitals and the IDF. Donation is voluntary and unpaid, donors get a free screening (blood pressure, haemoglobin, pulse), and O-negative is in constant demand. Do not quote a "percentage of IDF supply" or a "donations needed per day" figure: neither is published by MDA and both have circulated in write-ups without a source.

## Eligibility, from MDA's own donor page

- **First donation: ages 18 to 60.** Ages 17 to 18 require a parent's or guardian's written consent.
- A first donation over 60, or a repeat donation over 65, requires the treating physician's
  approval **renewed once a year**, requires the donor to meet the standard donor metrics, and
  must take place at a **fixed** donation site where a staff member certified in basic
  resuscitation is present. Not in a mobile caravan.
- At least **3 months** must have passed since the previous whole-blood donation.
- Weight must be over **50 kg**.
- Blood pressure must be between 100/60 and 180/100 mmHg.
- Menstruation is not a bar to donating.
- Anyone who has had a severe reaction to a previous donation, such as loss of consciousness or
  convulsions, is advised not to donate.
- Donors with a disability can email b101b@mda.org.il in advance to be advised by a blood-services
  professional and told which sites are accessible. Donors using crutches may donate at a fixed
  site with professional approval, not in a caravan.

The eligibility rules themselves are set by the Ministry of Health on the advice of a transfusion-
medicine expert committee, following FDA and European practice, not by MDA.

## Booking

- MDA Blood Services: **03-5300400** (this is also the number for organising a group blood drive)
- Email: hatramot_dam@mda.org.il

**Do not route a donor to 101.** That is the ambulance dispatch line and every call to it ties up
an emergency channel.
