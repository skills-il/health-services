# ER payment: the complete official exemption table

Source: Ministry of Health, `פטור מתשלום עבור ביקור בחדר מיון`
(https://www.gov.il/he/service/emergency-room-payment), page last updated 14.07.2026.
Amounts come from a separate document, the Ministry of Health tariff
(https://www.gov.il/he/departments/dynamiccollectors/moh-price-list), currently effective 1.7.2026.

Read this file before answering any question about what an ER visit costs. The list below is
**enumerated at the source**. If a condition is not on it, do not tell the user they are exempt on
account of that condition, however serious it sounds. But note the entitlements in the next section:
they sit outside the Ministry of Health list and are commonly missed.

**Verify against the live page before giving a definitive answer.** The Ministry of Health revises
this page, and a stale local copy that reads as closed will produce confident wrong answers.

## The two components

An ER visit is billed as two separate things:

1. `אגרת מיון` : the ER fee. Tariff code 00964, **281 NIS**.
2. `תשלום על הטיפול הניתן בחדר המיון` : the cost of the treatment given.

The global tariff for a general-hospital ER visit (code 5044) is **1,241 NIS**.

Exemptions come in two tiers, and the tier decides which component is waived.

## Tier 1: full exemption (both the fee and the treatment cost)

Available to members of a kupat cholim in these cases:

- Arrived with a medical letter or a `טופס 17` (a commitment form from the insuring kupah) **and was not admitted**
- **Was admitted** to hospital, even with no medical letter and no `טופס 17`
- Work-injury casualty presenting employer confirmation (National Insurance form 250)
- Road-accident casualty presenting police confirmation of the accident
- Pupil injured at school or on a school trip, holding school confirmation
- Victims of domestic violence (`נפגעי אלימות במשפחה`)
- Victims of sexual assault
- Referred to a general-hospital ER on the instruction of a district psychiatrist, or by court order in criminal proceedings, under the Treatment of Mentally Ill Patients Law
- Attended or was referred to an ER at a psychiatric hospital
- Evacuated to hospital by an MDA ambulance **from the street or another public place, following a sudden event**
- Attended for any of the following:
  - New fracture
  - Acute dislocation of a shoulder or other joint
  - Injury requiring closure by suturing or another closure method
  - Dislocated jaw
  - Foreign body aspirated into the airway
  - Foreign body in the eye
  - Snake bite or scorpion sting
  - Life-threatening allergic reaction
  - Head injury under age 2 or over age 70
  - Third-degree burn
  - Epileptic seizure in a known epileptic
  - Women in labour contractions
  - Cancer treatment
  - Haemophilia treatment
  - Cystic fibrosis treatment
  - Familial dysautonomia patients
  - Dialysis patients
  - Sudden onset of high fever (above 38.5C) in infants under two months

## Entitlements that sit OUTSIDE the Ministry of Health list

These are not on the MoH ER-payment page but they routinely apply, and reading that page as a
closed list turns their absence into an affirmative "you are not exempt":

- **Casualties of hostile action (נפגעי פעולות איבה).** Treatment costs are borne by the National
  Insurance Institute under the Victims of Hostile Action Compensation Law. This covers the acute
  stress and anxiety presentations that fill ERs after alerts, not only physical injury.
- **Soldiers** are treated at civilian ERs at the IDF's expense.
- **A patient sent to the ER by a Terem doctor or an after-hours kupah physician**, holding that
  referral, is in the same position as anyone arriving with a medical letter: full exemption if
  not admitted. This is the commonest real-world route to a full exemption and it is easy to miss
  because the referral did not come from the patient's own family doctor.

## Tier 2: treatment cost waived, the `אגרת מיון` still charged

- Attended between **01:00 and 06:00**, for any reason not listed under full exemption
- Found after the visit to have been **medically justified**, even if not on the full-exemption list, **regardless of the hour of the visit**

## Mental-health medical centres

`הפונים למחלקה לרפואה דחופה - מלר"ד (חדר מיון) במרכז רפואי לבריאות הנפש פטורים מתשלום.`
Attendance at an ER in a mental-health medical centre is free. Basis: `חוזר מנכ"ל 06/2024`.

## What is NOT on the list

These are commonly assumed to be exempt and are not. Do not tell a user they are covered:

- Asthma attack
- Poisoning or overdose as such (a snake bite or scorpion sting is listed; a general poisoning is not)
- Animal bites other than snake bite and scorpion sting, including dog bites
- Burns below third degree
- A blanket reduction for "children under 18" or "pregnant women". Only the specific rows above
  (head injury under 2, fever above 38.5C under two months, labour contractions) apply.

There is also **no 23:00 boundary** anywhere in the regulations, and no flat discounted night price.

## The route back for a self-referrer who paid

Bring the discharge summary and the receipt to your kupat cholim. If the visit is found to have
been medically justified, the kupah issues a `טופס התחייבות` and the treatment cost is waived
retroactively. The kupah is not obliged to issue one for a non-emergency or elective visit.

## If the tariff figure looks stale

The tariff is revised several times a year. Fetch the current file from the Ministry of Health
tariff collector and read row 5044 (`ביקור במלר"ד בבית חולים כללי, תעריף גלובלי`) and row 00964
(`אגרת מלר"ד`). Never carry an amount forward without checking its effective date.
