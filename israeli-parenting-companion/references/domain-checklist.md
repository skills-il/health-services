## Must cover (core)
- [ ] Tipat Halav visit calendar from birth date - 11 visits: birth, 1 month, 2 months (given at 6 weeks since 3.4.2022), 4 months, 6 months, 9 months, 12 months, 18 months, 24 months, 4 years, 5 years. No 3 year visit - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/infant-and-child-development/tipat-halav/tipat-halav-schedule/ - the core .ics deliverable
- [ ] Physician exam visits - "בדיקה רפואית" at 2, 9, 18 and 24 months; nurse at every visit - source: same tipat-halav-schedule URL - tells parents which visits are longer and what to prepare
- [ ] What happens at each visit - growth measurement (weight, length, head circumference), developmental check, guidance topics, vaccines, vision acuity at 4 years, first-grade readiness at 5 years, blood count referral at 9 months for the 12 month visit - source: same URL - "what to ask / check" column of the checklist
- [ ] Per-visit developmental checkpoints (parent-facing MoH list, 2m to 5y) quoted as "what the nurse checks", not as a diagnostic norm table - source: same URL - developmental-milestone part of the calendar
- [ ] Routine vaccine rows per age, as checklist items: birth HepB 1 + RSV; 1m HepB 2; 2m (6 weeks) 5-in-1 1, Rota 1, PCV 1; 4m 5-in-1 2, Rota 2, PCV 2; 6m 5-in-1 3, Rota 3, HepB 3; 12m 5-in-1 4, PCV 3, MMRV 1; 18m HepA 1 (+ MMRV 2 for the 2025 cohort); 24m HepA 2 (pre-2025 cohort only); grade 1 MMRV 2 (pre-2025) or HepA 2 (2025+); grade 2 DTaP-IPV booster + flu; grades 3 and 4 flu; grade 7 HPV 1; grade 8 Tdap + HPV 2 - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/babies-immunization-schedule/ and https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/luah/he/files_publications_units_epidemiology_vaccine_guidelines_120_LUAH.pdf - calendar must list each row
- [ ] Birth-cohort switch on 1.1.2025 - born 1.1.2025 or later: MMRV 2 at 18 months, HepA 2 in grade 1; born 31.12.2024 or earlier: MMRV 2 in grade 1, HepA 2 at 24 months. Effective at Tipat Halav from 1.7.2026 - source: LUAH PDF footnotes 2 and 3; https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/viral-diseases/measles/ - wrong cohort means a wrong calendar
- [ ] 2-month vaccines moved to 6 weeks "until further notice" since 3.4.2022 - source: LUAH PDF footnote 1; tipat-halav-schedule page - calendar date for the 2 month visit
- [ ] RSV passive immunization by birth month - born 1.9 to 31.3: in hospital before discharge; born 1.4 to 31.8: at Tipat Halav before the season; second season only for high-risk infants - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/children-vaccines/rsv-vaccine-beyfortus/ - birth-date dependent calendar item
- [ ] School vaccines are given by the school health service (nurse), with parental consent via the health statement, booklet requested about two weeks before - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/vaccine-schedule-6-to-18-years/ ; Kol Zchut "חיסונים לתלמידי בית ספר" - parents of 6 to 18 year olds
- [ ] HPV regional exception - Ashkelon district and Tzfat sub-district give both HPV doses in grade 8 - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/vaccines-out-of-tipat-halav/ - otherwise the calendar is wrong for those regions
- [ ] Missed vaccine catch-up routing - under 6: Tipat Halav nurse; up to grade 9: school health service; grade 10 to age 18: health bureau vaccine clinics via 5400*; from 18 (21 in special education) catch-up is paid; HPV free up to 26 - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/routine-vaccinations-completion/ ; gov.il service page "השלמת חיסוני שגרה לתלמידים" - the skill routes, never sets intervals
- [ ] Late doses still count, no restart; missing or doubtful records means "treat as unvaccinated" (MoH clinician rule, quote only as context, route to nurse) - source: https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/hashlama/he/files_publications_units_epidemiology_vaccine_guidelines_170_hashlama.pdf - answers the most common parent worry without giving intervals
- [ ] Olim children with a foreign schedule - MoH catch-up chapter explicitly targets "ילדים עולים חדשים"; bring the foreign record to the Tipat Halav nurse (under 6) or the school nurse; nurse maps it to the Israeli plan - source: hashlama PDF; gov.il page "שירות מונע בטיפות חלב" (olim as priority group) - required scenario
- [ ] Preterm babies: vaccines by CHRONOLOGICAL age, with two MoH exceptions (rotavirus after NICU discharge, HepB at 2 kg or 1 month or discharge, whichever first); flu from 6 months - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/infant-and-child-development/prematurity/preterm-infant-vaccination/ - calendar must NOT shift vaccines to corrected age
- [ ] Preterm babies: growth curves read by CORRECTED age (MoH); developmental checks also consider corrected age, mainly in the first year, up to 3 years for very small preemies (Maccabi only, no MoH end age found) - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/infant-and-child-development/tipat-halav/about-growth-charts/ ; https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/maccabi_circles/children/infants/preemies/development/ - milestone section must show both ages and defer to the nurse
- [ ] Red flags, call 101 or go to ER now: hard to wake, unresponsive or loss of consciousness; marked confusion; seizure; red or purple rash that does not fade on pressure; breathing difficulty or pale mottled skin with signs of severe illness - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/viral-diseases/meningitis/ - safety-critical routing
- [ ] Fever by age - under 2 months, 38 or above: ER or urgent care immediately (above 38.5 no referral needed and ER visit exempt from fee); 2 to 3 months, 38 or above: urgent medical check, ER if limp or unusually sleepy; 2 months to 1 year: watch drinking and behaviour, see a doctor if it does not pass in a day or two; any age: breathing difficulty or apathy means medical check; no improvement after 48 hours - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/fever/high-temperature-and-fever/ and meningitis URL - most common urgent question
- [ ] Seizure - call MDA if it does not stop within 5 minutes, or with breathing difficulty, choking or blue skin; lay on side, do not open the mouth - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/medical-conditions/convulsions/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Breathing distress in infants - more than 60 breaths a minute under 1 year, chest in-drawing between ribs, nasal flaring: immediate medical check or ER - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/respiratory-system/bronchiolitis/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Dehydration and GI red flags - signs (little or dark urine, dry mouth, no response, sunken fontanelle); vomiting over 24 hours; diarrhea over 48 hours in infants and toddlers, 72 in children; blood or mucus; fever above 39; bloody or green vomit means ER now - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/digestive-system/vomiting/ and .../diarrhea/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Non-urgent route: Tipat Halav nurse line "קול הבריאות" 5400* (extension 1 then 9), Sunday to Thursday 16:00 to 21:00, Friday 08:00 to 13:00, from abroad 972-8-6241010; kupa nurse line for referrals to ER - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/infant-and-child-development/tipat-halav/tipat-halav/ - where to send "is this normal" questions
- [ ] Poison or medicine overdose: National Poison Information Center 04-7771900, 24 hours - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/fever/antipyretic-therapy-in-children/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Shaken baby warning for crying - "בכי לא הורג, טלטול כן" - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/early-childhood-care/different-types-of-crying/ - crying is a top question
- [ ] Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months; continue breastfeeding to 2 years and beyond; no water needed under 6 months for breastfed babies - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/baby-nutrition/nursing/exclusive-breastfeeding/ ; MoH circular 6.4.2025 - feeding core
- [ ] Complementary foods around 6 months by readiness signs (interest in food, sitting, head control); not before 4 months; texture progression at 7 and 9 months; no cow's milk as a drink and no honey before 1 year (botulism) - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/baby-nutrition/solid-food/toddler-nutrition/ - solids core
- [ ] Choking hazards under 5: nuts, peanuts, almonds, seeds, popcorn, hard and gummy candy unless ground smooth - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/safe-environment/home-safety/prevent-choking/safe-baby-feeding/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Supplement timing without doses - vitamin D from birth to 1 year (same for preterm); iron from 4 completed months to 18 months, stopped at the 18 month visit. Doses: "ask the Tipat Halav nurse, follow the label" - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/early-childhood-care/vitamin-d-for-babies/ ; tipat-halav-schedule visit guidance - calendar reminders; skill gives no doses
- [ ] Safe sleep - back only; firm mattress with Israeli standard mark, no incline; only sheet and tucked blanket, no pillows or toys; crib in parents' room at least to 6 months, preferably 1 year; no bed sharing; pacifier; no smoking; supervised tummy time when awake - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/safe-environment/home-safety/sudden-infant-death-syndrome-prevention/ - safety-critical
- [ ] Sleep expectations - night waking to eat is normal in year 1; around 3 months 4 to 5 hour night stretches, 14 to 16 hours a day; MoH says online sleep-hour tables are not reliable per child; no screens 2 hours before bed at ages 2 to 5 - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/early-childhood-care/sleep/sleeping-habits/ - top everyday question
- [ ] Refusal pattern for splitting, delaying or skipping vaccines - quote MoH: splitting does not reduce pain, delays protection; route to the nurse - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/child-immunization-faq/ - hard rule of the skill
- [ ] When not to vaccinate today (dose-free MoH rule): acute illness with fever above 38 postpones; mild illness, a cold, mild diarrhea are not a reason to postpone; final call is the nurse's - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/vaccine-schedule-6-to-18-years/ - common "my baby has a cold" question
- [ ] First Tipat Halav visit - about an hour; bring cloth diaper, spare diapers and clothes, formula and bottle if not breastfeeding, vaccine booklet, hospital discharge documents - source: tipat-halav/tipat-halav URL - printable checklist item

- [ ] Regional school exceptions - South and North districts: grade-2 diphtheria-tetanus-pertussis-polio booster in grade 1; Ashkelon district and Tzfat sub-district: both HPV doses in grade 8 - source: babies-immunization-schedule page; vaccines-out-of-tipat-halav page - added after round-1 review
- [ ] Developmental escalation - published referral signs (Maccabi) and loss of skills route to the pediatrician now - source: https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/maccabi_circles/child_development/development-basics/developmental_delays/ - added after round-1 review
- [ ] Preterm hepatitis B series and iron from 2 weeks; NICU discharge letter governs - source: preterm-infant-vaccination and preterm-infant-feeding MoH pages - added after round-1 review

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)
- [ ] Measles outbreak overlay - extra early MMR dose at 6 to 11 months in outbreak localities, when visiting them, or before any trip abroad, in addition to the 2 routine doses; children over 1 with one dose bring dose 2 forward - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/common-childhood-diseases-and-symptoms/viral-diseases/measles/ - time-limited, mark as "check current status"
- [ ] Vaccines outside Tipat Halav - flu yearly from 6 months at the kupa; meningococcal B (Bexsero) recommended mainly for infants, paid at the kupa with referral; pertussis in pregnancy weeks 27 to 36 - source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/raising-children/immunization-schedule/vaccines-out-of-tipat-halav/ - parents ask about these
- [ ] Preterm hepatitis B with a carrier mother - vaccine near birth plus passive HBIG; dose before 2 kg not counted - source: preterm-infant-vaccination URL - edge case, route to NICU / nurse
- [ ] Digital vaccine booklet in gov.il personal area; complete from 15 March 2025, partial before, keep the paper booklet; lost booklet can still be vaccinated at school; reconstruction is paid - source: routine-vaccinations-completion URL; school vaccines URL - olim and lost-booklet scenarios
- [ ] Historical schedule items a parent may see in an older child's booklet - bOPV (oral polio) 2013/2014 to March 2025, stopped March 2025; PCV7 2009, PCV13 from Dec 2010, PCV20 now; HPV girls-only 3 doses until 2014, boys and girls 2 doses from 2015 - source: https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/policy/lohut/he/files_publications_units_epidemiology_vaccine_guidelines_140_LUHOT.pdf ; polio and pneumococcal vaccine pages - explains booklet differences without telling anyone to re-vaccinate
- [ ] Atopic family and allergenic foods - MoH: families with atopy should see a dietitian and a doctor before 6 months to plan complementary feeding; introduce allergenic foods one at a time over several days. No MoH early-peanut recommendation found; route specifics to israeli-food-allergy-navigator - source: MoH Nutrition Division letter 27.10.2021 (pediatrics.org.il copy) - parents will ask about peanut and egg
- [ ] Avoid formula in the first days if planning to breastfeed (allergy risk and supply); MoH does not recommend routine continued formula exposure for allergy prevention - source: MoH circular mch-115136725 (6.4.2025) - conflicting advice parents hear
- [ ] Water for formula boiled until 1 year; plant milks are not a substitute - source: MoH Nutrition Division letter 2021 - common bottle-feeding question
- [ ] Dental: free caries prevention at about 70 Tipat Halav stations from first tooth to age 6, booking 6362* - source: tipat-halav/tipat-halav URL - new service, calendar reminder
- [ ] Anaphylaxis - adrenaline injection then ER within a quarter of an hour - source: anaphylactic-shock URL - route, no dosing
- [ ] Where Tipat Halav is free and who can use which station (MoH stations serve everyone regardless of kupa, including foreign workers and women without status; TLV/JLM municipal stations serve their own residents; kupa stations mainly their members) - source: tipat-halav/tipat-halav URL - olim and non-residents
- [ ] Antipyretics exist from 2 months, under 3 months syrup only; skill gives no doses and points to the label and pharmacist - source: antipyretic-therapy-in-children URL - parents will ask "how much"; answer must route

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)
- [ ] Daycare subsidy, gan registration, child allowance, special-education committees - israeli-childcare-navigator - plausible question, route there
- [ ] Developmental diagnosis at child development institutes, paramedical therapy entitlements and copays - israeli-hmo-navigator (rights) - plausible after a missed milestone; skill says "ask the Tipat Halav nurse or pediatrician for a referral" and routes rights questions
- [ ] Kupa rights, ER fee exemptions beyond the MoH quoted rule, supplementary insurance for Bexsero or HPV over 26 - israeli-hmo-navigator - plausible, route
- [ ] Pregnancy care, pertussis vaccine in pregnancy details, IVF - israeli-fertility-guide - plausible, route
- [ ] Food allergy diagnosis, peanut early-introduction protocols, anaphylaxis action plans - israeli-food-allergy-navigator - plausible, route
- [ ] Meal plans and recipes for toddlers or picky eaters - israeli-nutrition-planner - plausible, route
- [ ] Diagnosing a rash, illness or developmental delay; prescribing; medication or supplement doses; deciding to skip, split or delay a vaccine - nobody (hard refusal, route to Tipat Halav nurse, pediatrician, or 101) - would be asked, must get a refusal plus a route
- [ ] Clinical catch-up intervals and minimum ages from the Tadrich tables - nobody (nurse decides) - would be asked by olim parents; answer is "bring the record to the nurse", not a computed schedule
- [ ] Teen mental health, adolescent sexual health beyond the HPV vaccine - israeli-mental-health-navigator (mental health) - plausible for 12 to 18; route

## Known bad figures (secondary sources that conflict with primary)
- Fever threshold age "under 3 months" (common in US content) vs MoH "עד גיל חודשיים" for immediate ER; MoH adds a 2 to 3 month "urgent check" tier on the meningitis page. Use MoH ages.
- Maccabi: every seizure needs urgent care / ambulance even if it stopped; MoH: call MDA if over 5 minutes or with breathing problems, most stop on their own. Use MoH for 101 trigger, still route any first seizure to a doctor.
- Kol Zchut "חיסונים לפגים" says RSV prophylaxis "ניתן בקופות החולים"; current MoH: universal RSV immunization in hospital or Tipat Halav by birth month. Kol Zchut is stale on this.
- Kol Zchut first visit "within two weeks of birth" has no MoH portal counterpart found; present as Kol Zchut guidance only.
- RSV season months differ inside MoH: "אוגוסט עד מרץ" (visit page, preterm page body) vs "ספטמבר עד מרץ" (preterm page summary, LUAH annex "מספטמבר עד 31למרץ"). Use the birth-month rule from the RSV vaccine page instead of season months.
- MoH Hep A vaccine page still lists only 18 and 24 months, contradicting the 2025-cohort rule (grade 1) on the schedule page, LUAH PDF and measles page.
- MoH catch-up Tadrich chapter (Feb 2017) still lists bOPV and PCV13; both superseded (bOPV stopped March 2025; PCV20 now).
- News reports (WebSearch summaries of Maariv, Kikar, Bhol) disagree on the effective date of the MMRV change (1.7.2026 vs mid August 2026); MoH measles page says "החל מ-1.7.2026".
- PCV20 routine start "February 2025" comes only from Maccabi via search summary; not confirmed on an MoH page.
