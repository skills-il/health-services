# Domain checklist: israeli-nutrition-planner (health-services)

Verification note (2026-09-19): all six Phase-2 red-label cells (solid/liquid x sodium/sugar/saturated fat) and the Phase-1 cells were matched against the rendered MoH Efsharibari legislation page; exemptions were matched against the regulation text on nevo.

## Must cover (core)

### A. Front-of-package red label (תקנות בריאות הציבור (מזון) (סימון תזונתי), התשע"ח-2017, in force 1.1.2020)
Values are per 100 g (solid) or per 100 ml (liquid); stage 2 applies since 1.1.2021. Each row is its own item.

| # | Nutrient | Form | Stage 1 (2020) | Stage 2 (from 1.1.2021, current) | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Sodium | Solid | 500 mg / 100 g | 400 mg / 100 g (verified live) | https://efsharibari.health.gov.il/en/governance/legislation/unhealthy-food-labeling-law/ |
| A2 | Sodium | Liquid | 400 mg / 100 ml | 300 mg / 100 ml (verified live) | same |
| A3 | Total sugars | Solid | 13.5 g / 100 g | 10 g / 100 g (re-verify) | same; nevo regulation Second Schedule |
| A4 | Total sugars | Liquid | 5 g / 100 ml | 5 g / 100 ml (re-verify) | same |
| A5 | Saturated fat | Solid | 5 g / 100 g | 4 g / 100 g (re-verify) | same |
| A6 | Saturated fat | Liquid | 3 g / 100 ml | 3 g / 100 ml (re-verify) | same |

- A7. Label wording and meaning: three separate red symbols "כמות גבוהה של נתרן / סוכר / שומן רווי"; a product can carry 0 to 3. Explain that "no red label" is not the same as "green label". Source: same page.
- A8. Exemptions from red labeling: single-ingredient unprocessed foods, and categories listed in the regulation (for example plain milk and unsweetened dairy with only lactose, infant formula and baby food, small packages below the minimal surface threshold, foods sold unpackaged). Must enumerate each exemption category from the regulation text, not summarise. Source: nevo, תקנות בריאות הציבור (מזון) (סימון תזונתי) התשע"ח-2017, תקנה on פטורים; https://www.nevo.co.il (search the regulation title).
- A9. Where the threshold applies: "as sold" vs "as prepared" for powders and concentrates (per the regulation's reconstitution rule). Source: nevo regulation; Efsharibari page.
- A10. Sweetener marking: products with added non-nutritive sweeteners; state what the current rule is only after checking the amended regulation (hedge otherwise). Source: nevo regulation amendments.

### B. Green label (סמל ירוק)
- B1. Voluntary, awarded by MoH per food category against category-specific criteria (whole grains, fiber, fat quality, sodium, sugar); criteria list updated periodically. Cite the current criteria document, do not hardcode old criteria. Source: https://efsharibari.health.gov.il/eat-healthy/buy-healthy-food/food-labeling/green-labeling/
- B2. A product cannot carry both a red label and a green label. Source: same.

### C. Nutritional Rainbow (הקשת התזונתית) and MoH dietary guidelines
- C1. Rainbow food groups and frequency framing (vegetables and fruit, whole grains and legumes, protein sources, dairy, fats; water as main drink), per the 2019 MoH national nutrition guidelines. Source: https://efsharibari.health.gov.il/en/ and MoH nutrition guidelines publication on gov.il.
- C2. Mediterranean pattern, limiting ultra-processed foods, sugary drinks, red and processed meat. Source: MoH 2019 guidelines.
- C3. Converting frequency guidance into a weekly menu without numeric calorie targets.

### D. Religious and calendar constraints
- D1. Kosher: meat/dairy separation and waiting interval by custom; pareve planning. Source: general halachic practice (cite kashrut authority, not MoH).
- D2. Halal planning for Muslim users.
- D3. Pesach: chametz exclusion, kitniyot by custom (Ashkenazi vs Sephardi), kosher-for-Pesach label reading and red-label still applying.
- D4. Fasts: Yom Kippur and Tisha BeAv (full day), minor fasts, Ramadan; pre-fast and break-fast meals; medical exemption cases (diabetes, pregnancy, medications) routed to physician. Source: MoH fasting guidance on gov.il (search "צום יום כיפור משרד הבריאות").

### E. Nutrient gaps and supplements
- E1. Documented Israeli gaps: vitamin D, iron (women, pregnancy), folic acid (pre-conception 0.4 mg), iodine (Israel has no mandatory salt iodization; MoH pregnancy iodine recommendation), B12 for vegans, calcium. Source: MoH Mabat survey and me.health.gov.il pregnancy pages.
- E2. Tolerable upper limits and drug interactions (vitamin K and warfarin, grapefruit and statins or others, calcium and levothyroxine or some antibiotics, potassium salt substitutes with ACE inhibitors or kidney disease). Source: MoH / pharmacist leaflets; route to pharmacist.
- E3. MoH routine supplement programs: vitamin D and iron for infants (Tipat Halav). Source: https://me.health.gov.il (infant vitamin D and iron).

### F. Pregnancy food safety
- F1. Listeria-risk foods (unpasteurised dairy, soft cheeses, deli meats, smoked fish), raw eggs and meat, high-mercury fish (swordfish, shark, king mackerel, tuna limits), caffeine limit, alcohol zero. Source: https://me.health.gov.il/parenting/family-planning/healthy-pregnancy/healthy-lifestyle/food-safety/
- F2. Folic acid and iodine supplementation (as E1).
- F3. Never set a pregnancy weight target; route gestational diabetes to kupa.

### G. Allergen and gluten labeling
- G1. Mandatory allergen declaration list under the Israeli pre-packed food labeling rules and "may contain" precautionary labeling. Source: MoH National Food Service; https://www.gov.il/he/pages/02012025-01
- G2. "ללא גלוטן" claim threshold (20 ppm) and celiac planning. Source: MoH food service gluten-free guidance.
- G3. Sesame (tahini) and nuts as prominent Israeli allergens.

### H. HMO dietitian entitlements (סל הבריאות)
- H1. Dietitian referral rules differ by kupa (Maccabi: no referral for diabetes, prediabetes or gestational diabetes; others: check with the kupa); co-pay per visit per kupa and level of supplementary insurance. Sources: Maccabi https://www.maccabi4u.co.il/eligibilites/3856/ ; Clalit, Meuhedet, Leumit equivalents; kolzchut "ייעוץ תזונתי" page https://www.kolzchut.org.il
- H2. National Diabetes Program and conditions covered (diabetes, celiac, obesity-related, pregnancy with GDM, kidney). Source: https://www.gov.il/he/pages/diabetesnationalproject

### I. Safety gates
- I1. Hard refusal of calorie, deficit, goal-weight, rate-of-loss or weight-loss macro numbers.
- I2. Eating-disorder screening signals and routing: family doctor, hospital ED clinics (referral), ERAN 1201 (https://www.eran.org.il/), emergency 101.
- I3. Kidney disease: withhold plan, route to renal dietitian.

## Should cover (advanced)
- S1. GLP-1 context (semaglutide, tirzepatide): protein and hydration focus, GI side effects, Sal Briut eligibility criteria for obesity indication as published by MoH; no dosing, no weight targets. Source: the current sal briut listing on gov.il (no specific announcement verified; skill routes to the kupa) and Sal Briut committee decisions.
- S2. Reading the Hebrew nutrition facts table per 100 g, per-serving values, and the sodium-to-salt conversion (salt = sodium x 2.5).
- S3. data.gov.il MoH nutrient composition database (Tzameret) for food lookup: https://data.gov.il/api/3/action/package_show?id=nutrition-database
- S4. Vegetarian and vegan planning with Israeli staples (legumes, tahini, fortified plant milks) and B12.
- S5. School and kindergarten food and sugary-drink policy (MoH circulars) for parents.
- S6. Food cost and budget planning with Israeli supermarket staples.
- S7. Older adults: protein, vitamin D, dysphagia routed to clinic.

## Out of scope (explicit)
- Clinical nutrition therapy, therapeutic diets (renal, liver, PKU, enteral or TPN).
- Eating-disorder treatment or meal plans for anyone with ED signals.
- Calorie targets, deficits, goal weights, macro splits for weight loss.
- GLP-1 or any drug dosing, starting, stopping or eligibility decisions.
- Sports and performance nutrition.
- Infant formula selection and complementary-feeding schedules beyond pointing to Tipat Halav.
- Halachic rulings (defer to a rabbi or kashrut authority) and halal certification rulings.
- Diagnosing allergies or intolerances.
