# Israeli Front-of-Package Food Labeling (Red/Green System)

Source: Israeli Ministry of Health, Efsharibari program
Official page: https://efsharibari.health.gov.il/en/governance/legislation/unhealthy-food-labeling-law/
Regulation: Approved by Knesset Health Committee, December 2017. Implemented in two phases.

## Phase 1: January 2020 (Initial Thresholds)

| Nutrient | Per 100g (solid food) | Per 100ml (liquid food) |
|----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Sodium | 500 mg | 400 mg |
| Total Sugars | 13.5 g | 5 g |
| Saturated Fat | 5 g | 3 g |

## Phase 2: January 2021 (Current Thresholds)

| Nutrient | Per 100g (solid food) | Per 100ml (liquid food) |
|----------|----------------------|------------------------|
| Sodium | 400 mg | 300 mg |
| Total Sugars | 10 g | 5 g |
| Saturated Fat | 4 g | 3 g |

**IMPORTANT:** Always use Phase 2 thresholds. Phase 1 is historical only. There is no
Phase 3 in the regulation.

## Red Labels (Mandatory)

- Red circle appears on the front of the package
- Contains the nutrient name (sodium, sugar, or saturated fat)
- A product can have 0, 1, 2, or 3 red labels
- Required for all packaged food products sold in Israel
- Exemption: packages with front area smaller than 25 cm2

## Green Labels (Voluntary, and NOT frozen)

The green label is the moving part of this system. Two operative rules govern it:

1. **A food that carries any red label can never carry a green label.** In the Ministry's
   own wording: "מזון שמכיל סמל אדום לפי חוק, לא יוכל להכיל סמל ירוק".
2. **Eligibility is restricted to an enumerated list of food groups**, not judged
   product by product: milk, yogurts, cheeses, tofu, soy beverages, certain vegetable
   oils, seeds and nuts, grains, legumes, tahini, tahini salad, fish, eggs, poultry,
   fruits and vegetables.

Other criteria set by the scientific committee: no red label; compliance with the maximum
sodium threshold set per food group where salt may be added; unprocessed, minimally
processed or processed under the NOVA classification with no preservatives or additives
beyond standard fortification; compliance with Israeli dietary guidelines; and a
meaningful nutritional contribution to the overall diet.

### The criteria are updated annually

Eligible products are reviewed by an independent professional scientific committee that
updates the criteria every year based on scientific advances, field inquiries, and public
health needs. **Do not describe the green label as static.** In February 2026 the
Ministry added a new eligible category, "Prepared Dishes and Salads", so that
ready-to-eat or packaged legume salads, vegetable- and whole-grain-based dishes, hearty
soups, and combined meals can carry the green label, provided every ingredient meets the
criteria.

Before asserting that a specific food is or is not green-label eligible, check the current
criteria on the Ministry page rather than relying on this file.

Current criteria page (HE): https://efsharibari.health.gov.il/eat-healthy/buy-healthy-food/food-labeling/green-labeling/

## Key Distinctions

1. Red labels mark EXCESS of specific nutrients, not overall nutritional quality
2. A product without a green label is NOT necessarily unhealthy
3. Fresh unpackaged produce (fruits, vegetables) carries no labels
4. The Israeli system is binary (red/green), unlike:
   - EU Nutri-Score (A-E scale)
   - UK traffic light system (red/amber/green per nutrient)
   - Chile/Mexico warning octagons

## Common Products by Label Status

### Typically Zero Red Labels
- Fresh produce (unpackaged)
- Plain yogurt, white cheese, labaneh
- Whole grains (bread, rice, pasta)
- Raw legumes and nuts (unsalted)
- Olive oil, tahini
- Fresh eggs, fish, poultry

### Commonly Carry Red Labels
- Sweetened breakfast cereals (sugar, sometimes sodium)
- Flavored yogurts and dairy desserts (sugar)
- Processed deli meats and sausages (sodium, saturated fat)
- Packaged snacks and chips (sodium, saturated fat)
- Sweetened beverages (sugar)
- Commercial sauces and dressings (sodium)
- Frozen prepared meals (sodium)

## Full Exemption List (no red label even if over threshold)

- Fresh produce (unpackaged fruits and vegetables)
- Fresh meat, fresh fish, fresh eggs sold raw and single-ingredient
- Restaurant and prepared foods bought at food-service establishments
- Multipacks, and packages whose front area is smaller than 25 cm2
- Specific exempted single-ingredient products: tea, coffee, yeast, food extracts

Exempt products must still show the standard nutrition facts panel on the back of the pack.

## Hebrew Label Vocabulary for Allergen Scanning

| Hebrew | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| רכיבים | ingredients |
| מכיל | contains |
| עלול להכיל | may contain (precautionary) |
| עלול להכיל שאריות של | may contain traces of |
| מיוצר במפעל המעבד גם | made in a facility that also processes |
| ללא גלוטן | gluten free |
| חלב, אבקת חלב, מי גבינה, קזאין | milk, milk powder, whey, casein |
| ביצים, אבקת ביצים | eggs, egg powder |
| חיטה, שיפון, שעורה, כוסמין | wheat, rye, barley, spelt |
| בוטנים | peanuts |
| אגוזים, שקדים, אגוזי לוז | tree nuts, almonds, hazelnuts |
| שומשום, טחינה | sesame, tahini |
| סויה, לציטין מסויה | soy, soy lecithin |
| דגים, פירות ים | fish, shellfish |
| חרדל, סלרי, לופין | mustard, celery, lupin |

Ingredients are listed in descending order by weight, so the first item is the largest
component. A precautionary "עלול להכיל" line flags possible cross-contact; its absence is
not proof that a product is free of an allergen.
