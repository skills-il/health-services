# Israeli Supermarket Staples and Population Nutrient Gaps

Companion reference for `israeli-nutrition-planner`. Consult when building a shopping
list, or when a user asks why a particular supplement keeps coming up in Israel.

## Part 1: Staples to Keep Stocked (typically zero red labels)

Available at Shufersal, Rami Levy, Yeinot Bitan, Victory, Osher Ad.

| Category | Products |
|----------|----------|
| Whole grains | Lechem maleh (whole-wheat bread), orez maleh (brown rice), bulgur, quinoa, oatmeal |
| Legumes | Canned or dried chickpeas (hummus gargirim), lentils (adashim), white beans (she'u'it lavan) |
| Vegetables | Seasonal produce: tomatoes, cucumbers, peppers, eggplant, zucchini, leafy greens |
| Fruits | Seasonal: citrus (winter), stone fruits (summer), dates, pomegranates |
| Healthy fats | Extra-virgin olive oil (shemen zayit), raw tahini, unsalted nuts, avocado |
| Dairy (unsweetened) | Plain yogurt (yogurt tiv'i), white cheese (gvina levana), labaneh |
| Protein | Eggs, fresh chicken, fresh fish, canned tuna (check the sodium label) |

Products that commonly carry red labels: sweetened breakfast cereals, flavored yogurts
and dairy desserts, processed deli meats and frozen schnitzel, packaged snacks,
sweetened beverages, commercial sauces and dressings.

## Part 2: Population Nutrient Gaps in Israel

These are population-level patterns, not a diagnosis. Any actual supplement plan belongs
to the user's doctor or kupat cholim dietitian. See the supplement-safety rules in
SKILL.md Step 8.5 before naming any dose.

### Iodine

Israel has no mandatory salt-iodization policy, and retail salt is not iodized by
default. Combined with heavy reliance on desalinated drinking water, which contains
essentially no iodine, this leaves a documented national deficiency. National
biomonitoring in 2020-21 found a median urinary iodine concentration of 64.5 micrograms
per litre in adults, below the WHO adequacy floor of 100.

Practical: prefer iodized salt where it is available. In pregnancy and lactation an
iodine supplement is commonly advised, but the dose comes from the kupat cholim, and
iodine is not appropriate for everyone (see the thyroid caution in Step 8.5).

### Vitamin D

Deficiency is common in Israel despite the climate, driven by modest dress, sunscreen
use, and indoor lifestyles. Dietary sources alone (fatty fish, fortified dairy, eggs)
rarely close the gap. The maintenance dose belongs to the treating doctor. The
tolerable upper intake level for adults is 100 micrograms per day, which is 4,000 IU.

### Iron

Iron-deficiency anemia is common in women of reproductive age, and a plant-heavy
Mediterranean pattern supplies iron in a less absorbable form.

Practical: pair a vitamin-C food (peppers, citrus, tomatoes) with a plant-iron source
(lentils, tahini, dark leafy greens) in the same meal. For per-food nutrient values,
use the Israeli national nutrition database (Tzameret) rather than a foreign database:
foreign tables mis-describe Israeli staples such as tahini, whose values differ by
whether the seed is hulled.

### B12 for vegans and vegetarians

Israel has an unusually large vegan and vegetarian population. No plant food is a
reliable B12 source, so a B12 supplement is required for anyone eating fully plant
based. Prefer calcium-fortified plant milks.

### Folate around pregnancy

Supplementation from pre-conception through the end of the first trimester is the
standard recommendation to reduce neural-tube defects. The routine dose is 400
micrograms per day. The 5 milligram dose used for higher-risk pregnancies is a
prescription-strength dose and is never self-selected.

## Part 3: Why This Matters More in Some Communities

The Ministry of Health's own regulatory impact assessment records overweight and
obesity rates in Arab society reaching 38% among children and 70% among women of low
socioeconomic status. The Ministry's Arab-community campaign records diabetes
prevalence 2.5 times higher than in Jewish society, and heart disease 2.3 times higher.
Meal planning for Arab-Israeli households is not an edge case, it is the highest-need
segment in the country.
