# Domain Coverage Checklist - foreign-caregiver-payroll

Generated: 2026-06-29 via research on Kol Zchut (aggregator) + Bituach Leumi + Population and
Immigration Authority sources. This file is the coverage contract the skill is judged against.

## Must cover (core)

- [x] Employment permit (heter haasaka) from the Population and Immigration Authority - required before employing - source: kolzchut/קבלת_היתר
- [x] Eligibility groups (85+, שר"מ below 112%, cancer, disabled child) - defines who may employ - source: same
- [x] Mandatory registration with a licensed private bureau - source: kolzchut/מדריך_לגיוס
- [x] Mandatory private medical insurance + deduction cap 168.80 NIS/month from January 2026 (154.29 is a January-2023 figure) - source: kolzchut/ניכויים_מותרים_משכר_עובד_זר
- [x] **The FULL permitted-deductions table**, not one row: a 25% overall cap on all discretionary deductions; medical insurance at half the premium up to the annual cap; housing as a reimbursement of expenses actually incurred, capped by REGION and building type from 1.9.2026 (utilities, furniture and appliances included, no separate utilities line, no employer-owned half rate), with the pre-September half-rate table and 94.34 utilities line kept only for back-pay; and the PROHIBITION on deducting any National Insurance from a nursing caregiver. Plus: an un-taken deduction cannot be taken retroactively, and over-deducting is a criminal offence - source: חוק עובדים זרים תשנ"א-1991 ס' 1ה(ב); תקנות עובדים זרים (שיעור ניכויים מהשכר בעד מגורים הולמים) תש"ס-2000 as amended by the 2026 amendment (ק"ת 12476, p. 2424, in force 1.9.2026); kolzchut/ניכויים_מותרים + kolzchut/ניכוי_הוצאות_מגורים
- [x] Minimum wage floor (monthly 6,443.85 NIS, hourly 35.40 NIS) - source: kolzchut/שכר_מינימום
- [x] No hourly overtime for live-in caregivers (Yolanda Gluten) - source: kolzchut/תנאי_העסקה
- [x] Weekly rest 25 hours; 150% for work during it - source: same
- [x] National Insurance 3.6% (employer), full wage, ceiling abolished - source: kolzchut/דיווח + btl/mekabelSiud
- [x] Register as employer with Bituach Leumi (household-employer track) and pay on BL schedule - source: btl/mekabelSiud
- [x] Deposit fund standard 12.5% (6% severance + 6.5% pension) - source: kolzchut/הפקדת_פיקדון
- [x] Deposit exemption is the INTERSECTION of the nursing sector AND employment by an individual otherwise than in the course of a business, not "private households" generally. What is owed instead: on resignation or dismissal within a year, 12.5% for months FROM THE SEVENTH; where severance is triggered, 8.33% from month 1 plus 6.5% from month 7. A foreign worker cannot be insured in an Israeli pension fund, so nothing is withheld from the worker - source: kolzchut/תשלומים_במקום_ביטוח_פנסיוני + kolzchut/הפקדת_פיקדון
- [x] Severance = one last-month salary x years worked (about one twelfth/month; reserve the full amount, not 6%) - source: kolzchut/זכותון
- [x] Written employment contract in the worker's language at the start - source: kolzchut/תנאי_העסקה
- [x] Monthly payslip (tlush sachar) issued every month - source: kolzchut/תנאי_העסקה
- [x] Sick days: accrue 1.5/month, cap 90, paid 0 / 50% / full - source: kolzchut/דמי_מחלה
- [x] Live-in housing deductions DO apply when the worker lives in the patient's own home, inside the 25% cap. From 1.9.2026 the cap is the regional permanent-building ceiling (Jerusalem 1,095, Tel Aviv 1,257, Haifa 999, Centre 1,030, South 999, North 999) and covers utilities; it limits a reimbursement of expenses actually incurred, and how that is measured in the family's own flat is NOT settled, so the skill routes a deduction above the old figures to a labour lawyer - source: the 2026 amendment + kolzchut/ניכוי_הוצאות_מגורים. Updated 2026-10-19: the employer-owned half rate and the 94.34 utilities line this row carried were repealed on 1.9.2026
- [x] Termination: severance + advance notice (up to one month) + pidyon chufsha + final payslip; on patient death severance still owed, bureau relocates worker - source: kolzchut/זכותון
- [x] Income tax: at minimum wage generally below threshold, no tik nikuyim; National Insurance is the main withholding
- [x] Havraa tariff 451.50 NIS/day private sector, havraa-year 2026, updated 18.08.2026 with RETROACTIVE effect to 1.7.2025 and a duty to pay differentials on havraa already paid at 418 - source: kolzchut/דמי_הבראה. This figure moves mid-year; re-read it every cycle rather than carrying it forward
- [x] Havraa days BY the year of employment being worked (1st=5, 2nd-3rd=6, 4th-10th=7, 11th-15th=8, 16th-19th=9, 20th+=10), NOT by completed years: a worker with one completed year accrues 6 days - source: same (its worked example pays "6 ימי הבראה עבור השנה השנייה"). Corrected 2026-10-19; the skill, script and checker had keyed it on completed years
- [x] Havraa becomes PAYABLE after 12 months but is then paid retroactively from day one, so it ACCRUES from month 1 and must be reserved monthly - source: kolzchut/דמי_הבראה ("לאחר שהשלים העובד שנת עבודה (12 חודשים) אצל המעסיק, הוא זכאי לתשלום דמי הבראה רטרואקטיבית, החל מיום עבודתו הראשון")
- [x] A dismissal shortly before the first anniversary is presumed made to avoid severance, so it does NOT cap exposure at 12.5% - source: Severance Pay Law 5723-1963, the provision headed "when dismissal does not prejudice rights"
- [x] Employer must provide accommodation for at least 7 days AFTER employment ends - source: Foreign Workers Law s.1E(a)
- [x] Register with Bituach Leumi within two weeks of starting employment - source: kolzchut/מדריך_להעסקת_עובד_זר_בסיעוד
- [x] On the patient's death the worker is treated as dismissed (Severance Pay Law, the provision headed "an employer who died") and is paid from the estate, whose bank account may be frozen pending a probate order - source: kolzchut/זכותון_עובד_זר_בסיעוד_שפוטר_או_שמעסיקו_נפטר
- [x] Annual leave has a NET and a GROSS column; redemption runs on the net figure - source: kolzchut/חישוב_מספר_ימי_החופשה_השנתית...6_ימים
- [x] Annual leave BY CALENDAR year of employment (s.1: the work year starts 1 January, so a mid-year starter moves up a row each January), 6-day week gross (years 1-5=16, 6th=18 ... 14th+=28) per s.3(a) - source: kolzchut/חישוב...6_ימים + the statute. Havraa, by contrast, runs on anniversary years
- [x] 9 holidays a year, by the worker's own religion or Israeli, their choice, fixed once - source: kolzchut/תנאי_העסקה
- [x] Nursing benefit, ALL SIX levels in BOTH columns, from 01.08.2026. Foreign caregiver: 1,708 / 2,484 / 3,478 / 4,471 / 5,465 / 6,458. Israeli caregiver: 1,708 / 2,484 / 4,223 / 5,217 / 6,458 / 7,452. 6,458 appears in both at different levels (until 31.07.2026: 1,705 / 2,480 / 3,472 / 4,464 / 5,456 / 6,448 foreign) - source: btl.gov.il/benefits/Long_Term_Care/Pages/money.aspx

## Should cover (advanced / edge cases)

- [x] Net monthly cost = gross cost minus the recipient's nursing-benefit level
- [ ] Per-year vacation proration thresholds (200/240 days) - omitted to keep the calculator simple
- [ ] Public-sector havraa tariff and bands - out of scope (private household is private sector)

## Out of scope (explicit, with rationale)

- Caregiver employed by a care company as the company's employee - the company carries the deposit/payroll duties
- Other foreign-worker sectors (construction, and the non-nursing sectors generally) - different permit regime and different deposit treatment. Note the deposit rationale is NOT "every other sector is in the fund": kolzchut/הפקדת_פיקדון lists the experts sector, AGRICULTURE and Palestinian workers as also outside it. The reason this skill is scoped to nursing is the permit, benefit and deduction regime, not the deposit fund alone. Corrected 2026-08-27
- Israeli (non-foreign) caregivers - standard hourly-overtime law applies; Gluten does not
- The non-binding "+20% live-in supplement" - it is a dissent, not law; explicitly NOT presented as an entitlement
- Income-tax withholding mechanics for the household employer - not captured to a quotable primary source this pass. Re-litigated 2026-10-19: a user may ask, and the skill answers at the level that matters (usually nil at minimum wage, confirm credit points with the Tax Authority); a full withholding walkthrough stays out of scope
