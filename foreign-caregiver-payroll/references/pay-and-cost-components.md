# Pay and Cost Components

Every rate and table used to compute the monthly cost of a foreign caregiver employed in a
private home, with sources. Figures are current as of mid-2026; verify against the linked
pages before quoting for back-pay or a dispute.

## Wage floor

| Item | Value | Effective |
|------|-------|-----------|
| Monthly minimum wage (full-time) | 6,443.85 NIS | 01.04.2026 |
| Hourly minimum wage | 35.40 NIS | 01.04.2026 |

A live-in caregiver is paid a monthly global wage. The Hours of Work and Rest Law does not
apply (Yolanda Gluten ruling), so there is no hourly overtime. The often-cited "minimum wage
plus 20%" is a non-binding dissent, not a statutory entitlement.

Weekly rest is 25 continuous hours; work during the weekly rest is paid at 150%.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/שכר_מינימום and
https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תנאי_העסקה_של_עובד_זר_בסיעוד_המועסק_בבית_המטופל

| Component | Rate / cap | Notes |
|-----------|------------|-------|

## Deposit fund: the home-employer exemption

The standard foreign-worker deposit is 12.5% of wages (6% in lieu of severance + 6.5% pension)
into a central fund. A nursing patient who employs the caregiver in their own home is EXEMPT
and pays the worker directly at the end of employment.

What the home employer owes directly is pension (6.5%) plus severance. Severance equals one
last-month salary multiplied by the number of years worked. The 6% "in lieu of severance" inside
the fund's 12.5% fully discharges severance only when held in a formal pension-fund arrangement;
a home employer paying in cash has no such arrangement, so the worker can claim full statutory
severance (one month per year), which is higher than 6%. Reserve pension 6.5% plus the full
severance accrual (about one twelfth of each month's wage), and top up after any raise because
severance is computed on the last salary.

## Sick days (dmei machala)

The worker accrues 1.5 sick days per month (18 a year), up to a maximum of 90. The first day of
illness is unpaid, the second and third days are paid at 50% of wage, and from the fourth day
onward the full wage is paid.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/דמי_מחלה

## Holidays by country of origin

The caregiver is entitled to pay for 9 holidays a year and may choose their own religion's
holidays instead of the Israeli holidays. The choice is made once and is fixed for the whole
employment.

Source: https://www.kolzchut.org.il/he/תנאי_העסקה_של_עובד_זר_בסיעוד_המועסק_בבית_המטופל

## Long-term-care (nursing) benefit offset

Paid in 6 levels. Monthly cash amount runs from 1,705 NIS at level 1 to 6,448 NIS at level 6
(as of 01.04.2026). The recipient may employ a foreign caregiver directly (cash) or through a
care company. Subtract the relevant level from the gross monthly cost to estimate net cost.

Source: https://www.btl.gov.il/benefits/Long_Term_Care/Pages/ovedZar.aspx
