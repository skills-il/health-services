# Kupah Physiotherapy Routes and Co-payments

Consult this file for a Tier 4 (routine) pain or injury, after you have ruled out Tiers 1
to 3 in `safety-red-flags.md`. It says how to reach basket physiotherapy at each fund and
roughly what it costs. It is routing information only: you do not examine, diagnose, or
prescribe rehab.

Checked against each fund's own pages on 2026-10-07. Funds change these pages, so tell the
user to confirm with their fund before relying on a detail.

## Referral: who needs one

| Kupah | Referral for basket physiotherapy |
|---|---|
| Maccabi | Not needed at Maccabi's own physiotherapy institutes; the user can book directly. A doctor's referral is still required for therapists and institutes under contract with Maccabi, and for an exercise consultation for diabetes, hypertension, or a cardiac condition. That last exception is about the exercise-consultation service, not about physiotherapy for, say, a knee in a member who happens to have high blood pressure. |
| Clalit | A referral is required, from a doctor or in some cases from a specialist nurse (community, rehabilitation, geriatrics, wounds and stoma). |
| Meuhedet | Meuhedet lists physiotherapy among its institutes and states that every visit to an institute needs a Meuhedet doctor's referral and a commitment (התחייבות) from the clinic secretariat. Its price list speaks of physiotherapy treatments "approved for performance". Tell the user to get both from their clinic before booking. |
| Leumit | A referral from the treating doctor is required; appointments are booked through the customer-service line. |
| Private | Outside these basket routes. If the user wants a refund from their supplementary insurance (שב"ן), the plan's own conditions apply and may include a referral, so check the plan before booking. |

Use the `israeli-hmo-navigator` skill for the mechanics of getting seen at the user's fund.

## Co-payment (השתתפות עצמית)

Basket physiotherapy is not free. In 2026 each of the four funds charges a quarterly
co-payment of about 41 NIS: Clalit 41 NIS (its quarterly institute fee), Maccabi 41 NIS,
Meuhedet 41.00 NIS, and Leumit 41.10 NIS. It is a quarterly charge, not a per-treatment
one, but how the quarter is counted differs:

- Clalit: for community institutes the fee covers every visit in the same profession for
  three months from the first visit, whichever institute and however many visits.
- Maccabi: charged per institute, so visiting two physiotherapy institutes in the same
  quarter means paying twice.
- Leumit: its price list states the quarterly charges per floating quarter (רבעון צף).

The amounts are updated during the year, so quote them as "about 41 NIS a quarter, check
your fund's current price list".

Some groups are exempt or capped. Clalit, for example, exempts a visit linked to a work
accident or a road accident (with a doctor's confirmation) and several benefit groups.
Send the user to their fund's exemptions page, or to `israeli-hmo-navigator`, rather than
deciding eligibility yourself.
