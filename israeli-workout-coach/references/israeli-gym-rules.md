# Israeli Gym Rules: Health Declaration, Medical Certificate, New Trainees, and Minors

Sources: חוק מכוני כושר (רישוי ופיקוח), תשנ"ד-1994 (Fitness Centres (Licensing and
Supervision) Law, 5754-1994), sections 3, 4 and 4א, in the consolidated text as amended by
Amendment 4, תשפ"ה-2024 (ס"ח 3329, 10.12.2024); and תקנות מכוני כושר (רישוי ופיקוח) (הצהרת
בריאות ותעודה רפואית), תשע"ו-2015, which fix the wording of the declaration form.
Consult this file when onboarding a user, when a user asks what the gym is going to ask
them for, whenever the user is training in a gym for the first time, or whenever the
trainee is under 18.

This is the paperwork side of the Step 1 health screen in SKILL.md. The screen itself is
what lets you coach safely; the rules below are what the user's gym is legally obliged to
collect. Tell the user about them, do not collect them yourself, and never present yourself
as a substitute for any of it.

## Health declaration (הצהרת בריאות), section 4(א)

A fitness centre may admit a trainee only after the trainee supplies a medical
questionnaire including a signed health declaration confirming they do not suffer from
medical problems liable to endanger them while training at the gym.

> מכון כושר יקבל מתאמן רק לאחר שהמציא לו שאלון רפואי הכולל הצהרת בריאות חתומה בידו המאשרת
> שאינו סובל מבעיות רפואיות העלולות לסכנו בשעת אימון במכון כושר

The minister may exempt classes of gyms from requiring one, so a specific venue may not ask.

### What the form asks

The wording is fixed by the 2015 regulations (First Schedule). Part A has eight yes/no
questions, paraphrased here:

1. Has a doctor told you that you have heart disease?
2. Do you get chest pain at rest, during everyday activities, or during exercise?
3. In the past year, have you lost your balance because of dizziness (answer no if the
   dizziness came from over-breathing, including during hard exercise), or lost
   consciousness?
4. Has a doctor diagnosed asthma, and in the last three months did you need medication for
   it or have shortness of breath or wheezing?
5. Did a first-degree relative die of heart disease, or of sudden death at an early age
   (before 55 for a man, before 65 for a woman)?
6. In the past five years, has a doctor told you to exercise only under medical
   supervision?
7. Do you have a chronic illness not listed above that may prevent or limit you from
   exercising?
8. For pregnant women: was this pregnancy, or any previous one, defined as high-risk?

What this means for the user, before they sign up:

- Any YES means a doctor's certificate is needed (below). There is **no age cut-off** in the
  law or the form. The form's closing note recommends graded, instructed exercise
  especially for someone over 45 who plans unaccustomed high-intensity activity, but that
  is advice, not a trigger.
- High blood pressure, diabetes and medications are **not asked by name**. They fall under
  question 7 only if the condition may prevent or limit exercise. Do not tell the user how to
  answer: tell them question 7 exists, and that if they are unsure whether their condition
  counts, their doctor is the one to ask, which is also where the certificate would come from.
- Instruction ג at the top of the form tells the trainee to consult a doctor about continuing at the gym after
  any change in their medical condition. That matches the safety tiers in SKILL.md: after an
  exertional faint or new exertional chest symptoms, the user goes back to the doctor before
  training again.

## Medical certificate (תעודה רפואית), section 4(ב)

If the trainee answers YES to any question on the health declaration, the gym may admit
them only after they also supply a doctor's certificate confirming there is no risk to the
trainee's health in training at a gym.

> השיב המתאמן בחיוב על אחת השאלות בהצהרת הבריאות, יקבל אותו מכון הכושר רק לאחר שהמציא לו
> גם תעודה רפואית שבה רופא אישר שאין סיכון לבריאותו של המתאמן באימון במכון כושר

## Renewal, section 4(ד)

- Health declaration: the gym must require a fresh one **once every two years**.
- Medical certificate: for a trainee who had to supply one, the gym must require a fresh
  one **once a year**.

> מכון כושר ידרוש ממתאמן שימציא לו הצהרת בריאות אחת לשנתיים, ולעניין מתאמן שהמציא תעודה
> רפואית, ידרוש ממנו מכון הכושר להמציא לו תעודה רפואית אחת לשנה

## New trainees: instructor induction, section 4א (Amendment 4, 2024)

A gym may admit a **new trainee** (someone who declared at registration that this is their
first time training in a gym) only after an instructor has given them guidance that
includes both a training plan tailored to them personally and the gym's safety rules,
including how to operate the machines.

> מכון כושר יקבל מתאמן חדש רק לאחר שהעביר לו הדרכה בידי מדריך הכוללת את כל אלה:

Whenever no instructor is present in the gym, a person who has completed a first-aid course
and holds a valid certificate must be present in the compound (section 4א(ב)). Section 4א does
not apply to a gym in a residential building, a workplace, or a hotel that is not open to the
general public (section 4א(ג)).

**What this means for you.** A first-time gym-goer is entitled to that instructor
induction, so tell them to declare that they are new and take it. If they also want this
skill's program, it complements the induction rather than replacing it: suggest they show
the instructor the plan and their `health_flags` limits.

## Minors (קטין = under 18)

**Parental consent, section 4(ג).** Where the trainee is a minor, written consent from one
parent to training at the gym, signed by that parent, is attached to the health declaration
or the medical certificate as applicable. The consent may be given for a single training
session or for types of training.

> היה המתאמן קטין, תצורף להצהרת הבריאות או לתעודה הרפואית, לפי העניין, הסכמה בכתב של אחד
> מהוריו לאימון במכון כושר חתומה בידי ההורה

"הורה" in the law includes a guardian and anyone with lawful custody of the minor
(section 1).

**Instructor for minors, section 3(ב)(1).** Whenever minors are training at a gym, an
instructor qualified to train minors must be present, and only that instructor may teach a
minor how to operate the equipment and supervise their training.

> בכל עת שקטינים מתאמנים במכון כושר יהיה נוכח במקום מדריך לאימון קטינים, והוא בלבד יהא
> רשאי ... ללמד קטין את דרכי הפעלתם של מכשירים במכון הכושר ולפקח על אימונו

A "מדריך לאימון קטינים" is an instructor whose certification under the Sports Law shows
they completed suitable training for instructing minors at a gym. The minister may further
set the maximum number of minors one such instructor may supervise at a time, age limits
per training type or equipment type, and safety rules for training minors (section 3(ב)(2)).
These are set in תקנות מכוני כושר (רישוי ופיקוח) (אימון קטינים במכון כושר), התשס"ה-2005;
among other things a child under 6 may not train in a gym (unless in rehabilitation, accompanied by their
physiotherapist), and the regulations cap how
many minors one such instructor may supervise at a time.

> מכון כושר לא יאפשר לקטין שלא מלאו לו 6 שנים להתאמן במכון כושר

**What this means for you.** A minor asking this skill for a program is not just a
"program conservatively" case. Say plainly that the gym needs a signed parental consent and
that a specifically qualified instructor has to be the one showing them the machines. Do not
occupy that role.

## No general duty to have an instructor on the floor

Until December 2024, section 3(א) required an instructor to be present whenever a gym was
operating. Amendment 4 repealed it (section 3 is now titled "presence of an instructor for
training minors"), and the same amendment added the new-trainee induction and the
first-aider rule in section 4א above. So do not tell a user that an instructor is legally on the floor at all
times. "Ask an instructor to check your form on a new or heavy lift, if one is on duty" is
still good advice; it just is not a legal guarantee.

> סעיף קטן (א) -בטל;
