# Domain checklist: israeli-oncology-navigator (health-services)

Prepared 2026-09-19. Scope: bureaucracy navigation for adult and pediatric cancer patients and their caregivers in Israel. Rate figures are 2026 values; all BTL amounts are indexed each 1 January and must be re-read from the rate pages on every update.

Verified live on 2026-09-19 via curl: BTL oncology hub page (50% שר"מ for six months on medical documents for listed drugs; NI-contribution exemption at 75%+ pension; income-tax exemption at 90% for 185+ days). Other items cite the pages already used in the skill's evidence.json; items marked "section unverified" were not opened section-by-section in this pass.

## Out of scope (explicit)

| Item | Rationale (2026-09-19) |
|------|------------------------|
| Clinical treatment advice, regimen choice, prognosis | Medical practice reserved to licensed physicians; skill routes, never prescribes. |
| Drafting court pleadings or appeals to the Labour Court as legal representation | Reserved to advocates under חוק לשכת עורכי הדין; skill describes the route and refers to lawyers / Patients' Rights Association. |
| Quoting specific שב"ן or private-insurance caps and country lists | Change per tier and year; must be read from the current תקנון. |
| Private health / critical-illness insurance claims | Contract-specific, separate insurer regulation; out of navigator scope. |
| Nursing (סיעוד) benefit for post-retirement-age patients in depth | Covered by the elder-care navigator skill. |
| Cancer in non-residents / tourists | Not covered by National Health Insurance Law; different regime. |
| Alternative / unproven "cure" treatments | No authoritative Israeli entitlement; risk of harm. |
